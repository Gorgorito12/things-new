You were right about turretArc. Your setup is your own uhc.dll with our dinput8.dll beside it, and our dinput8.dll
reads its keys only from engine-ext\engine-ext.cfg and its data files only from engine-ext\. It never reads
Startup\wol.cfg or data\uhc\. A line in wol.cfg did nothing.

What changed:
- Every pack README now says engine-ext\engine-ext.cfg (or engine-ext\<file>) first. Startup\wol.cfg and data\uhc\
  are named only for the case where our UHC.dll replaces yours. Packs fixed: zamburak, gatling-camel,
  age5-shipments, auto-scout, editor, faith, hammer-fanning, mapuche, me-herdables, min-ui (also minuirows.xml),
  mustard-gas, oil-panel, options-menu, politician-ui, research-on-the-move, select-fattened, special-transforms
  and veterancy-marks. The Spanish halves are fixed too.
- The apply_fix.py scripts now choose the file for you: engine-ext\engine-ext.cfg (and engine-ext\ for data files)
  when the game folder has an engine-ext folder, and Startup\wol.cfg / data\uhc\ otherwise. --check reads the
  file they chose, and --restore undoes it. culture-ui's --engine-ext flag still works. Command names still go in
  data\uhc\customprotounitcommands.xml, because your own DLL reads that file.
- The zamburak and gatling camel packs still do not edit the cfg. Put this one line in engine-ext\engine-ext.cfg:
    turretArc GatlingCamel art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_A55.aim GatlingCamelDeployed art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_B55.aim MEZamburak art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\zamburak_cannon\zamburak_cannon.aim
