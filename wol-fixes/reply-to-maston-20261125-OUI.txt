#81 Observer UI: the interface files (draft, waiting for our approval of the pictures)

Your DLL loads its own interface files for a local observer: uimainnewobserver<W>x<H>.xml at 7 resolutions
(plus the jp twins) and seven ...observer.xml panels. They were made from the older interface that is still
inside data\Data3.bar, which is why the observer saw the resource column one row off (no oil), your own
resource panel under the observer's player rows, and the stock game's flags.

We rebuilt all of them on your current files (uimainnew.xml and the minimized panels), keeping ESOC's panel
exactly as it was laid out at each resolution:
- every gadget the panel code of the 36 observer maps uses is there (checked for all 36 maps and every
  resolution);
- your normal minimized UI no longer shows under the observer's panel: your resource panels, the command,
  socket, treasure and unit stat panels and your top score panel are hidden for the observer (the observer
  has no economy or units), and the minimap art stops at the minimap;
- the flags are your civs' flags (one per civ in civs.xml, the same numbering the repaired maps use);
- the home city button (the observer uses it to see the players' decks) sits at the left edge above the
  minimap, where nothing covers it at any resolution or in 3v3;
- your Menu > Icons row and building search row, if shown, sit below the observer's top band.

New resolutions: we also made 2560x1080, 1920x1200, 1440x900, 1600x1200, 2560x1440, 3440x1440 and
3840x2160. ESOC's panel is drawn in pixels (fixed fonts, 49 px icon cells), so on a bigger screen it keeps its
1080p size, centred between the minimap and the unit panel. Two things are needed on your side:
1. your uhc.dll has a list of the 7 resolutions it swaps files for; these new files are only used if you
   add them to that list (or tell us and we will do the swap in another way);
2. 2560x1080 works as it is. The taller ones (1200, 1440 and 2160 lines) each come with a preset in the
   observer maps (a load<WxH>() with more line breaks in the panel's text), so the shipment counters stay
   under the flags and the rows line up; checked in game at every one but 3840x2160, which our test PC could
   not drive.
