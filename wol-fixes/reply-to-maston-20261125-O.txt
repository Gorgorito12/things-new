Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-25: the observer panel in the 36 maps (#81)
==============================================================================

Tested in single player in two copies of WoL 1.0.15c with your own uhc.dll. The new observer interface files
(all resolutions) are in the other part of this reply.


What changed in the 36 maps
---------------------------
The 36 maps that carry the ESOC observer panel (the 30 ESOC_* maps, NEWMagdalena and the five vividly* maps)
are repaired by one script, wol-fixes/observer/observer_maps.py (apply / check / revert, originals kept). With
the Observer box unticked, each repaired map generates exactly as before: we generated every map with the same
seed at 3, 4 and 8 players, original against repaired, 108 times, and got the same units at the same places
every time.

With the box ticked, the panel now uses your data instead of the numbers of the stock game:
- Oil is shown in the fourth resource row of each player, with your oil icon (it showed export, which your
  own resource panel does not show either). All four display modes work for it.
- A player in the Great War shows your age-6 icon. The Great War does not change the engine's age (it stays
  at Imperial), so the panel looks for the civ's Great War tech (GreatWarButton).
- Your units, techs and home city cards are in the panel's tables, about 7,400 rows in all, generated from
  protoy.xml, techtreey.xml, civs.xml and the homecity files. So the unit rows show your villagers, military,
  ships and buildings, and "Techs Completed" and "Shipments Sent" show your own techs and cards with their
  icons.
- The panel now watches all 10,542 of your techs (it only looked at the first 2,550).
- Each observer used to get two techs by number: in your techtree those numbers are
  PoliticianQuartermaster and IndustrializeSpanish. They are now the two ESOC meant: no XP trickle, and Spies
  (the observer sees the whole map).
- Revolutions, consulate techs, age-up progress and the shipment progress bar use your techs and civs.
- The flags use your civ numbers, and the new interface files have a flag for every civ.
- The six taller resolutions of the new interface files have their panel settings in the maps.

The script must run again after a release that changes techtreey.xml, protoy.xml, civs.xml or the home city
files; "check" tells you when that is needed. Every player in a game needs the same maps.

Cost: the panel's code is bigger. On ESOC Arizona with 4 players and 2 observers, the game took about 12 s
longer from Play to the start (63 s instead of 51 s on our PC), and the observer's frame rate went from 114 to
103 fps. The panel only runs on the observer's PC, so the players' frame rate does not change.


How it was tested
-----------------
- All 36 maps as 1v1 + 1 observer, 2v2 + 2 and 3v3 + 2, each observer an AI in the last slots, in a team of
  its own: no script error in any of the 108 games, each observer had only its own unit, and every real
  player got a start, with one exception from the map itself: on ESOC Great Basin in 3v3, one of the six
  fixed 3v3 start points gives no town, so one player starts with nothing (a different player each time).
- King of the Hill (3 maps): one hill, no error.
- Save and load: the games load and go on.
- One team defeated: the game ends at once with victory for the other team, with the observers still there.
- The panel itself, by making our own player an observer for the test: oil, the Great War icon, your units,
  techs and cards with their icons.
- Not tested here: a human observer. That needs a second game joining a multiplayer lobby, so your DLL's own
  observer part (the file swap, removing the observer's units) did not run in our tests. Please check it in
  one of your games: the lobby's Observer box ticked, the teams in order, the observers in the last slots.

Two things we found in WoL itself
---------------------------------
1. With WoL in Spanish, an AI whose name has an accent ("Carlos el Diplomático", "Napoleón III" and 11 more)
   stops the whole trigger script of the map: the map then has no triggers at all (King of the Hill, the
   observer panel, your own triggers). The cause is wotta_triggers() in RM4\triggers\triggerLoader.xs: its
   messages ("... has entered the Great War Age!") put the player's name inside the text, and the script
   compiler refuses the accented letter. We saw it on ESOC Adirondacks; any map that calls wotta_triggers()
   would do the same with such a name. We did not change that file, since 193 maps use it. Would you like us to
   prepare a change?
2. In a single-player skirmish your DLL reads the Observer box from a profile setting
   (gamecampaignshownhcnote). It was off in every profile we have, and we did not see anything in normal play
   turn it on; but if it were on, a skirmish of 3 or more players on one of these 36 maps would make the last
   slot(s) observers. Your DLL could read the box only in multiplayer games.
