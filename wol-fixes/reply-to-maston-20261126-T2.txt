Hi JTMaston,

The gatling camel (#102), round 6: the deployed camel's arc now has its bisector perpendicular to the camel's
spine, as you asked.

1. GatlingCamelDeployed: the gun across the camel
- Deployed, the gun rests pointing straight out over the camel's right side (+90). It turns 60 degrees each
  way of that, from +30 (forward-right) to +150 (back-right): a 120-degree arc opening on the right side.
- The gunner stays on the opposite side, the camel's left, behind the gun with both hands on the two long
  handles. He steps round the post with the gun, at stations every 30 degrees, one foot at a time; when the
  gun stops, the step under way finishes, so no foot is left in the air.
- A target beyond the arc makes the camel turn until the target sits at +90 (its right side toward the
  target); then the gun turns the rest of the way. Before, the camel turned to face the target.
- Deploying: the camel lies down, the gunner gets off on its left, takes the handles and swings the gun out
  to +90 while he steps round the post. Undeploying: he swings it back to the front, gets on, and the camel
  rises. The times are unchanged (5.33 s and 4.17 s).
- Lying down, the camel turns its neck to its right, so its head is out of the gunner's way.
- The reload is the same as before (he walks in to the breech, throws the empty magazine aside, draws a full
  one from his pocket, seats it and walks back), with the gun at +90.
- Killed while deployed, the camel no longer rolls over (the barrels would have gone into the ground): it
  slumps and lays its head down, the gun still across it, and the gunner falls backward, away from it.
- If you'd rather have the arc on the camel's left side, that is a mirror of all of this (the gunner would
  get off on the right), so say so and I'll make it.

2. Firing while mounted
- Noted: you'll lock GatlingCamel's firing from the saddle behind a card in your data. Nothing changes on our
  side; GatlingCamel is the same as before, file for file.

3. The DLL key (turretArc), the same line in Startup\wol.cfg:
    turretArc GatlingCamel art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_A55.aim GatlingCamelDeployed art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_B55.aim
- The deployed camel's aim file now says where the gun rests (+90). The key keeps the gun within the <turret>
  number each side of that rest (60 in GatlingCamelDeployed.tactics, so +30..+150; you can narrow it there),
  and turns the camel so a target beyond the arc ends up at +90.
- Please use the new DLL with this pack. The new aim file is version 2: an older DLL refuses it (turretArc is
  then not applied). The deployed camel also needs the key: without it the game turns the camel to face its
  target, and the gun would point to the side.
- The code for WoL's DLL is in the porting pages (turretArc).

Everything is in wol-fixes/gatling-camel (apply_fix.py with --check and --restore; README in English and
Spanish).
