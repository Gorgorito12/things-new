Hi JTMaston,

The gatling camel (#102), rewritten: the round-4/5 art is now in the pack and replaces every art file of the
earlier versions.

1. GatlingCamelDeployed: the gunner on foot, behind the gun
- The camel lies down, the gunner gets off, side-steps back along its flank and works the gun on foot from
  behind it, like a man behind a pintle gun. Both hands are in front of his chest on two long handles: a
  crank for his left hand (it turns the gun's crank through a shaft) and a traverse bar for his right.
- The gun turns 100 degrees a second, from 30 degrees toward his side to 90 degrees across the camel. He steps
  round the post with it, one foot at a time; when the gun stops on its target, the step he is in finishes,
  so no foot is left in the air. A target beyond that arc makes the whole camel turn, as today.
- To reload he walks in to the breech, throws the empty magazine aside, takes a full one from his pocket,
  seats it and walks back to the handles (3 s, as before).
- Deploying takes 5.33 s and undeploying 4.17 s (GatlingCamelDeployed.tactics, the animation lengths). Its
  <turret> line is now maxTurnAnglePerSecond="100" so his steps keep pace with the gun.

2. GatlingCamel (from the saddle): the same unit as before, with the fixed art
- The earlier art had two faults, now fixed in every animation: the gunner's forearm could turn over in one
  frame, and at the jog and the run the gun slid off its post.

3. The DLL key (turretArc), one line in Startup\wol.cfg, unchanged:
    turretArc GatlingCamel art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_A55.aim GatlingCamelDeployed art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_B55.aim
  The key now keeps each gun inside the angles its aim file covers (-30..+90 for the deployed camel, -90..+90
  from the saddle), so the same <turret>90</turret> tag gives each unit its own arc. Please use the new DLL
  with the new pack: an older DLL would let the deployed gun turn to -90 and leave the gunner's feet mid-step.
  The code for WoL's DLL is in the porting pages (turretArc).

Everything is in wol-fixes/gatling-camel (apply_fix.py with --check and --restore; README in English and
Spanish).

Tested in game (single player, a test copy of WoL with the new DLL and pack): deploying, firing at targets all round
(the gun turns from -30 to +90; a target beyond turns the camel until it is inside the arc), the reload walk, the
gun coming to rest in the middle of a step (his step finishes, both feet down), undeploying and getting back on,
deaths mounted and deployed (the camel rolls over with the gun on it, he falls beside it), and the saddle unit
firing, reloading and dying. Every animation also passed our automatic checks before it went into the game.

Videos: gatlingCamelDeployed-wol.gif (the gunner on foot: deploy, aiming, reload, undeploy),
gatlingCamel-wol.gif (from the saddle), turretArc-wol-control.gif (the same scene with the key off: the whole
camel turns), gatlingCamel-move-wol.gif (both units walking).
