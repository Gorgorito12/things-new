Hi JTMaston,

Hammer fanning: thanks for the answers. Balance is yours, the same animation for both abilities, and retarget on
both: that is what the pack already does, so nothing changes there.

The muzzle flash at the hand: fixed.

What was wrong: the six flash tags of the new animation still named the game's "musketshot" particle set, and that
set draws its flash on the right hand. With the outlaw's old clip it looked fine. With the new clip, the pistol is
held by its grip at the hip, so the flash showed at the hand, beside the grip, not at the barrel.

The fix (in the same pack, wol-fixes/hammer-fanning):
- a new particle set, "fanningshot", added to art\effects\particlesets_wol.xml: the same four musket particles as
  musketshot (same look), drawn on the bone Prop2;
- the two clips now keep Bip01 Prop2 on the pistol's muzzle. All the explorer models have that bone, and nothing
  else uses it in these animations;
- the six flash tags of RangedAttackSharpshoot name fanningshot.
apply_fix.py --check shows the new set, and --restore puts particlesets_wol.xml back as it was, like the other files.

Tested in a copy of WoL (single player):
- British Explorer, Hammer Fanning on six outlaw guardians: six shots 0.28 s apart, six dead. Every flash leaves the
  tip of the barrel along the shot's line, never at the hand.
- Danish Explorer (the older model): Slip Hammering on ten guardians, six shots and six dead; then Hammer Fanning on
  the four left, four shots and four dead. The flashes are placed the same way.
- The animation itself is unchanged. The AI still plays.
- SharpshootIdle is never seen in game: after an ability's attack the explorer goes back to his normal idle. We left
  it in. It is harmless.

Pictures: c1-ingame-flash.jpg in the pack (British above, Danish below, the flashes at the muzzle), and the updated
video in the guide, powerShots-wol.gif.
