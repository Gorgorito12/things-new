Paraguayan works (#69): wol-fixes\paraguayan-works\
--------------------------------------------------
"We believe it must be caused by the bone skeleton."
The skeleton is fine: the chimney bones are the arsenal's, in the same places and at the same scale. There were three
faults:
- Smoke: the works use effects\smoke\factory_smoke.particle. Its puffs are a quarter of the size of the arsenal's and live
  half as long, so on these chimneys it is a thin grey wisp that is usually not seen. The arsenal's own white_smoke_b
  alone was not enough either (it is not always active: the age-2 works showed no smoke for a whole minute). The fix adds
  works_smoke.particle, which is white_smoke_b made always active. The result is the arsenal's plume. Tell us if you want
  it lighter.
- Damage: in the six age 1-2 damage models, 14 of the 35 debris pieces were named "debris_1a.001" and so on. The arsenal's
  damage template never found them, so they never broke off. They are renamed now. goldworks.xml pointed to a damage file
  that does not exist (WoL\buildings\works\med_arsenal_age2.dmg). It now uses the arsenal's, like the other two works.
- Collapse, age 3: the works' age-3 models have the arsenal's bones turned another way, so the arsenal's collapse
  animation laid the chimneys on their side and left crates floating. The bones now have the arsenal's orientation.
  The standing building looks the same, and it now collapses upright.
WoL-paraguayan-works-fix.zip and WoL-paraguayan-works-originals.zip unzip over the WoL folder. Before/after videos are in
media\. The Heavy Works use other models and were not checked. Low model detail is not tested yet.
Note for modders: an anim XML saved with Unix (LF) line endings made the building invisible in our tests. Keep CRLF.

Mustard gas (#75): key truckAttackResourceFix, wol-fixes\mustard-gas\README.txt
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It is an engine fault, not your data. The gas attacks with the stock TruckAttack action, the monster truck's. Each hit
on an enemy unit sets that unit's whole resource inventory to 0, so a Mill, plantation or rice paddy under the gas is
empty for the rest of the game ("Current Gatherers: 0", still so 6 minutes later). We tried 25 data variants of the gas:
each one still emptied the Mill or did no damage. POWERtornado, POWERIncendio and the monster truck do the same.
With the new key, a building hit by a TruckAttack keeps its resources. The damage is unchanged (352 against 357 HP/s on
a test target).
