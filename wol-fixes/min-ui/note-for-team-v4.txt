Minimized UI, version 4: what changed after your feedback
=========================================================

1. Why the icons looked thin and soft
   It was not the font size. Our first icons were 16x16 pictures with 1-pixel strokes, soft (anti-aliased)
   edges and a grey outline, and the panel draws every icon at about 10 x 14 screen pixels. Shrunk that much,
   a 1-pixel stroke becomes a faint line and the soft edge becomes a blur. Your own icons next to them
   (stat_icon_ranged, _range, _attack, _building) are 32x32 uncompressed pictures with solid shapes, strokes of
   4 to 7 pixels and a black outline, so they stay bold at that size.
   The new icons are made the same way: 32x32 uncompressed (BGRA, one level, like yours), solid shapes, hard
   edges (every pixel fully shown or fully transparent), a black line around each part and a 2-pixel black
   outline. Round parts are drawn a little wider than tall, because the panel squeezes icons sideways.
   Redrawn: the stopwatch (rate of fire), the hourglass (train time), the eye (line of sight), the winged boot
   (speed) and the shield (resists). New: the revolver cylinder (projectiles, below).
   Before and after, enlarged 4 times from the game: final\minUIStats-v4-icons-x4.png.

2. Hit points as a word
   The heart is gone. The row reads "Hitpoints: 100/100", the word in the yellow of the panel's own labels and
   the value in white, as the original panel writes it. The word comes from the game's string table
   (string 68852), so it follows the player's language: "Puntos de Vida: 1200/1200" in the Spanish table, which
   fits. In minuirows.xml it is written [string:68852]; any row can use [string:ID] now.

3. The house
   Population uses the game's own house again (ui\ingame\stat_icon_building, your copy). Our drawn house
   (stat_icon_pop.ddt) is no longer in the pack.

4. Projectiles before the reload (the revolver cylinder)
   An attack that fires more than one projectile before it reloads shows that number right after its rate of
   fire, with the cylinder icon:   Ranged ATK:  11 (ranged) 3.50 (stopwatch) 4 (cylinder) 1 (area) 14 (range)
   The number is (shots before the reload) x (projectiles per shot), and it shows only when it is above 1.
   It comes from the attack's own entries in the tactics file:
   - numberprojectiles: projectiles per shot (your tactics already have it on 94 attacks: the Escopetero's
     volley 4, the Bacamarteer 6, the Schwarzlose 12, the floating battery's long-range attack 5, the explorers'
     Sharpshooter 3, ...);
   - NumberOfShots: shots before the reload, from our DLL's numberOfShots key (none in your data yet).
   Important: the panel only reads these numbers. Neither the game nor your current uhc.dll reads
   numberprojectiles, so today these units still fire one projectile per shot. With our DLL, the key
   "numberOfShots projectiles" makes them fire that many; without it, the number in the panel says what the
   data asks for, not what happens in combat. Tell us if you prefer the number to show only when that key is on.

5. Three and four costs
   Tested: Mehal Sefari (50 food, 50 gold, 50 trade), Mamluk (100 / 200 / 100), Carretero (110 food, 10 wood,
   10 gold), Sebastopol (200 / 600 / 600), Fort Wagon (1000 / 1000 / 1000): all on one line over the portrait.
   The Palace Caravan's four costs (600 / 600 / 200 / 200) did not fit the line; they now go on two lines,
   two and two, using the population line, which a unit with four costs does not have (in your data no unit
   with population has more than three costs). The Marechaussee costs only 150 wood in your current data (its
   food and gold costs are commented out in protoy.xml), and the panel shows that.

Everything else is as in version 3. The pictures and the full test table are in final\README.txt.
