2. "Remove the parenthesis from around icons"
   Two kinds of parentheses were in the tooltip:
   - ours: "Attacks every 3.50 s (stopwatch)" and "4 projectiles before the reload (cylinder)". Removed: the lines now
     end with the icon.
   - the game's, from your string table:
         42226  "%1s (%2s) Damage"                         -> "11 (sword icon) (Ranged) Damage"
         42229  "%1.1fX Bonus Damage against %2s (%3s)"    -> "1.5X Bonus Damage against (icon) (Maneuver Unit)"
         42230  "%1dX Bonus Damage against %2s (%3s)"      -> the same with a whole number
         42223  "Resists %1d%% of %2s (%3s) Damage"         -> "Resists 20% of Ranged (shield icon) Damage"
     The DLL already rewrites these tooltips for the minimized panel, so it now also drops the parentheses around an
     icon and around the name right after an icon: "11 (sword icon) Ranged Damage", "1.5X Bonus Damage against (icon)
     Maneuver Unit". Your string tables are not changed, and tipParentheses="keep" in minuirows.xml keeps them.
     Spanish reads "Dano de 11 (icon) A distancia" (final\minUIStats-v6-spanish-tip-escopetero.jpg).
     If you want the same everywhere (the big panel too, and without our DLL), these are the edited strings for
     stringtabley.xml (and the same change in each translation):
         42226  %1s %2s Damage
         42229  %1.2fX Bonus Damage against %2s %3s
         42230  %1dX Bonus Damage against %2s %3s
         42223  Resists %1d%% of %2s %3s Damage
     Spanish (ES-LA): 42226 "Dano de %1s %2s", 42229 "%1.2fX de dano adicional contra %2s %3s",
     42230 "%1dX de dano adicional contra %2s %3s".

   No machine gun or Chu Ko Nu has NumberOfShots. Pictures: final\minUIStats-v6-chukonu.jpg, -tip-chukonu-ranged.jpg,
   -tip-gatling-bombard.jpg, -tip-schwarzlose-bombard.jpg.

   So the burst of these units comes from their animation: each Attack tag of the attack anim is a full shot, with the
   attack's full damage, and the panel cannot know it from the tactics. Our suggestion: write the burst in the tactics
   as numberprojectiles (Gatling and organGun units 6, Gatling Camel 6, gwMaxim 12, Machinephant 16, Chu Ko Nu 3). Today
   nothing in combat reads numberprojectiles (neither the game nor your DLLs), so it only changes what the panel shows:
   "6 projectiles" for the Gatling, "3 projectiles" for the Chu Ko Nu, which is what they fire. Only if you later turn
   on our numberOfShots key with its "projectiles" option would combat multiply by it (a Gatling would then fire 6 x 6),
   so with that key the anim should keep one Attack tag. The Schwarzlose already does this: numberprojectiles 12, and 12
   tags in its anim. Tell us if you would rather have the panel count the anim's Attack tags by itself.
