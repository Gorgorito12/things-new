Minimized UI, version 8: your unit type icons
=============================================

(2026-11-23, later: version 9 draws every icon of the minimized panel square, so these icons are no longer widened
and the Minie fills the icon's height; its pictures replace final\minUIStats-v8-icons-*.jpg, the control excepted.
See note-for-team-v9.txt.)

Your four icons are in the pack, converted to the game's format. They were not redrawn: the art is yours, pixel for
pixel, only reduced to the icon's size.

    Line         (AbstractHeavyInfantry)   blue coat, the musket upright in front
                 art\ui\ingame\stat_icon_heavy_infantry.ddt
    Skirmisher   (AbstractRangedInfantry)  green coat, the musket held diagonally (new in the pack; it replaces
                 your current green skirmisher)
                 art\ui\ingame\stat_icon_skirmisher.ddt
    Standing     (AbstractInfantry)        purple coat, no weapon
                 art\ui\ingame\stat_icon_infantry.ddt and art\WoL\ui\ingame\staticons\stat_icon_standing_unit.ddt
    Maneuver     (AbstractRangedCavalry)   the winged Minie; the bullet tinted to #C75A21 (the colour of the
                 "Maneuver Unit" name), keeping its shading: the lit face is #C75A21, the shaded side and the bands
                 darker, the highlight lighter; the white wings and the dark outline as you drew them
                 art\WoL\ui\ingame\staticons\stat_icon_ranged.ddt

They go at the paths the panels already draw, so your attackTypeIcons line and your data do not change. All are
32x32 uncompressed BGRA, like your own icons. apply_fix.py installs them with the rest of the pack (and keeps your
files for --restore).

How they were fitted:
- The panels draw these icons 11 x 17 pixels at 1920x1080 (narrower than they are tall), so each picture was widened
  first: in game it keeps the proportions you drew.
- The three soldiers have the same scale, standing on the bottom edge (the Line soldier is drawn a little smaller
  in your art, so it stays a little smaller). Each icon is centred.
- The Minie is wide, so in game it is about 11 x 6 pixels: you see a copper bullet with white wings.

Which file is the Skirmisher: for AbstractInfantry and AbstractHeavyInfantry the game uses its own icon files
(ui\ingame\stat_icon_infantry, ui\ingame\stat_icon_heavy_infantry), whatever attackTypeIcons says; that is why
Standing goes in both files. For Skirmisher it uses only your line (AbstractRangedInfantry 42032
ui\ingame\stat_icon_skirmisher): with that triple taken out, the Lancer's "2x" against Skirmisher is not shown at all.

Checked in game (1920x1080, our UHC.dll, the pack installed with apply_fix.py and removed with --restore):
- Escopetero: 1.5x Maneuver (the Minie), 1.25x Line, 0.75x Scout, in the rows and the tooltip, both interfaces;
- Falconet in Bombard: 3x Standing, both interfaces;
- Lancer: Hand ATK 2x Skirmisher, row and tooltip (minimized interface; your big interface shows only the first
  attack row, Siege, so the hand row is not there);
- the rows end where they ended before (the icons have the same size).
