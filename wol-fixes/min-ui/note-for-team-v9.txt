Minimized UI, version 9: every icon square
==========================================

The icons of the minimized panel no longer look squeezed. This covers yours and the game's: damage types, rate of
fire, range, area, armor, the multipliers' unit types, and the cost, LOS and speed icons at the top.

Why they were squeezed: in the game's text markup <icon="(N)(texture)">, N sets the icon's WIDTH in pixels (N + 1)
and its height is always the height of the text line, which comes from the font. The game's own minimized rows
write (10), so at 1920x1080 every icon there was 11 pixels wide in a 17 pixel line: 0.65 of its width. That is why
the stopwatch, the shield and your soldiers looked thin.

What changed:
- Our DLL writes N = line height - 1 for every icon of the minimized panel, so each icon is drawn square, at its
  true aspect. The line height depends on the font size the game picks for the resolution (FONTS\fonts3.xml): a
  17 px line on screens 960 px tall or more (1280x960, 1920x1080, 2560x1440...), 14 px below (1024x768, 1600x900).
  We measured both in game.
- Your four icons are no longer widened. The three soldiers keep one scale. The Minie keeps its copper bullet and
  its wings as you drew them, shortened where they run straight (the stepped tips kept), so it fills the icon's
  height like the soldiers.
- Our six drawn icons (stopwatch, hourglass, shield, eye, winged boot, cylinder) are drawn round instead of wide.
- Your house icon (population, Buildings) is enlarged from 28 to 32 texels so it is as tall as the others. The
  pack replaces ui\ingame\stat_icon_building.ddt, and apply_fix.py keeps yours for --restore.
- A row too long for the panel still drops its last multipliers with "(+N)". The DLL now counts each icon at its
  real width, so nothing runs off the panel.
- The big interface is the game's own panel and does not change.

Checked in game with our UHC.dll, the pack installed with apply_fix.py and removed with --restore:
- the Escopetero, the Falconet in Bombard, the Lancer and the Villero (Koygua), rows and tooltips;
- 1920x1080 and 2560x1440: every row fits;
- 1600x900: every row fits;
- 1280x960 and 1024x768: the longest rows end with "(+2)" or "(+3)", nothing runs off the panel.
Pictures: final\minUIStats-v9-*.jpg (one per unit at 1920x1080, the tooltips, the big interface, and one picture
per resolution: -r768, -r900, -r960, -r1440). The review sheet: icons-v9-square-review.png.

To install over version 8: run "python apply_fix.py <WoL folder> --restore" first, then
"python apply_fix.py <WoL folder>", and use our new UHC.dll.
