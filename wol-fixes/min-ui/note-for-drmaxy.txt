The minimized UI (UiUnitselectionMIN) shows what the big UI shows, and more, with the DLL key minUIStats.
This note is for your information only: nothing in it needs an answer. README.txt has the full map, the

Layout: centred selection and symmetric rows
--------------------------------------------
You wrote that the selection was no longer centred. The cause: our first file took buttonArea from the
original game file (369 632 607 666, centre x 488), where yours has 369 697 669 666 (centre x 519, the
panel's centre is 521). The game centres the unit portraits in buttonArea, so ours moved them left. The
file now keeps your buttonArea, and every other original gadget as your file has it, except where the
icons below need a change (README.txt, "The original gadgets", lists each one and why).

The new layout, following your point and JTMaston's:
- The top band holds only the portraits of the selection, centred as in your file. Nothing of ours is in
  it (JTMaston: "the upper section should be kept as originally intended for the group icons").
- Mirrored around the panel's centre: over the portrait, the cost (resource icons and amounts, no "Cost"
  word) and the population (a house); right of the player's name, the train time (an hourglass) and the
  garrison. The unit's name and the player's name get the same width either side of the centre, each
  with one row centred under it: what the unit is doing (Done in, Builds, Regen, Overheal, Poison, Aura)
  and its line of sight (an eye) and speed (a boot).
- The stats keep your panel's two columns (x 410 and x 583): hit points as a heart and the value,
  resists as a shield and the value, then the attack rows, the 4th on next to the first three.
- The health bar under the portrait, as in your file: it shows only inside the unit's name, so it stays
  there, as you have it.
- Pictures side by side, your current panel and this one: media\minUIStats-v3-vs-wol-units.jpg,
  -groups.jpg and -more.jpg. The rectangles with the centre lines: media\minui-preview-proposed.png.

What follows JTMaston's points
------------------------------
1. Rate of fire: in each attack row, right after the damage, with a stopwatch icon:
   "Ranged ATK:  23 (ranged) 3 (stopwatch) 12 (range), 3x (cavalry)". The value comes before its icon, as
   the damage and range already do in the row. The icon is our own drawing (DE's art cannot be used).
2. Population: the game's house icon (ui\ingame\stat_icon_building, your copy of it) and the number.
3. No separate range row: the range stays where the attack row already shows it.
4. Cost: over the portrait, before the name, icons and amounts only.
5. and 6. The top band is only for the portraits.
Also from JTMaston:
- Only the attacks the unit can use. Crack Shot, Sharpshooter, Swashbuckler and the like are actions of
  the tactics file that only an ability uses; the game's big UI lists them on every unit of that tactics
  file, usable or not. With the key, the minimized UI shows such an attack only while one of the unit's
  abilities (abilities.xml) gives it: its tech is active and its age is the player's. A Presidial shows
  Sharpshooter (its own ability) and no Crack Shot; the Chief (WOLcartographer) shows Anti-artillery
  Crackshot only after HCSteelCrown. The "+2" of the first version came from those attacks; it is gone in
  the tests, and when rows still do not fit it now reads "+2 more" (the tooltip lists them).
- Icons instead of words: hit points (a heart), resists (a shield), line of sight (an eye), speed (a
  boot), train time (an hourglass). Each row keeps a tooltip with the word. The boot is the game's light
  infantry icon (ui\ingame\stat_light_infantry). The spy eye is ui\ingame\stat_icon_informers in the
  game, but your copy of that file is a dove, so the eye is ours; so are the heart, the shield, the
  hourglass and the stopwatch (16x16, the size and format of the game's stat icons, in art\ui\ingame).
  All icons are drawn at the size of the panel's own damage type icons.
- With 3 resistances (multiarmor), the resists value lists them all: "0.30 (hand)  0.20 (ranged)
  0.10 (siege)".

Earlier decisions (unchanged)
-----------------------------
1. The rows are top-level gadgets, never inside a gadget the game shows or hides, so a row never
   disappears with another one (your Speed text was inside the resists value).
2. Building armor: shown in the original resists spot, like for units. With your own uhc.dll (its
   multiarmor writes the resists text) the key leaves building armor to it: the first version showed
   "Resists: 0 (null)" on a Factory there; now buildings show no resists row in that case, as before.
3. Speed is the live speed (with techs, snares and auras), as the big UI shows it.
4. The attacks past the third, and the ones the big UI itself has no room for (it stops at 9 rows),
   are shown, in the same format.

Two things we noticed in your current files
-------------------------------------------
- In your uiunitselectionmin.xml, unitselectionpanel-unitTypeName (line 40) has no "/>" and no closing
  tag, so nearly the whole panel ends up as its child, and the extra "</gadget>" at line 88 closes the
  panel. The game copes with it. One part of it matters: the health bars show only because of it (they
  show with the name). Our file puts the bars inside the name on purpose and nothing else.
- Your uiunitstatpanel3.xml (the big UI's stat panel) holds the original minimized stat panel: 5 rows
  instead of 9, so the big UI shows only one attack row. The original is in optional\.

Everything is in the data file
------------------------------
Which gadget shows which item, with which icon, label, tooltip and for which unit types, which original
labels become icons, whether the attack rows get the ROF and the ability filter: all in minuirows.xml
(data\uhc\minuirows.xml). "[icon:path]" in a text puts any texture there at the panel's icon size. You
can move, remove, rename or add rows, change labels, icons or widths, without a new DLL.
