Minimized UI, version 5: answers to JTMaston's four points
=========================================================

1. "The num of projectiles should only be shown if it is higher than 1"
   It already was: the cylinder number shows only above 1. The "1" you saw is not a projectile count: it is the
   attack's area, the number right before the red target icon ("11 (ranged) 1 (target) 14 (range)"). It comes from
   <DamageArea>1.000000</DamageArea> on the Escopetero's ranged attack in protoy.xml, and the game writes it itself,
   in both panels, with or without our DLL (the tooltip says "14 Range with 1 Area"). Your current panel shows it too:
   final\minUIStats-v5-wolmin-escopetero.jpg. We kept it, since it is your data; say so if you want area 1 hidden.

2. "The Escopetero should show 4 (numberprojectiles)"
   Done. The number now comes from the tactics file as written: NumberOfShots x numberprojectiles. With any DLL the
   Escopetero shows 4, the Bacamarteer 6, the explorers' Sharpshooter 3 (final\minUIStats-v5-escopetero.jpg,
   -bacamarteer.jpg, -explorer.jpg).
   What the game does today is different, and you should know it: WoL's own uhc.dll fires ONE projectile per
   Escopetero volley. We traced it in the game with your uhc.dll (c070d138) and none of our keys: 4 volleys, 4
   projectiles launched; in a second fight 114 shots, 114 projectiles (final\minUIStats-v5-wol-volley-count.jpg).
   Nothing reads numberprojectiles today, neither the exe nor your DLLs. Our DLL's key "numberOfShots projectiles"
   makes combat fire the 4 (tested in version 4). If you prefer the panel to show what combat fires instead of the
   data, one attribute in minuirows.xml does it: projectiles="combat" on <bigPanelRows>.

3. "x1.3 should be x1.25, x0.8 should be x0.75"
   Found the cause. The game writes a multiplier with one decimal (the text ", %.1fx %s" inside age3y.exe), so 1.25
   becomes 1.3. Your uhc.dll changes that one character to 2 decimals; our UHC.dll, used in its place, did not, so
   our pictures showed 1.3x. New key "multiplierDecimals" (2 decimals by default) does the same as yours, and more:
   - the tooltips now agree. With your DLL the row says 1.25x but its tooltip still says "1.3X Bonus Damage against
     Line Unit" (the tooltip uses string 42229 of your string table, "%1.1fX ...", one decimal); the key gives the
     tooltip the same decimals: "1.25X" (final\minUIStats-v5-wolmin-tip-escopetero.jpg is yours today,
     -tip-escopetero-ranged.jpg is version 5). You could also just edit string 42229 to "%1.2fX Bonus Damage against
     %2s (%3s)" in stringtabley.xml (and the Spanish one), and the key would not be needed for the tooltip.
   - in the minimized rows the zeros at the end go: 1.50x is written 1.5x, so the long rows do not get longer;
     1.25x stays. (multiplierZeros="keep" in minuirows.xml keeps them.)
   - beside your uhc.dll it sees the 2 already there and changes nothing in that text.
   Also: your panel lists three multipliers for the Escopetero's ranged attack and ours listed two. The missing one was
   1.5x against Maneuver units (AbstractRangedCavalry). The game only shows multipliers against its own list of types
   with an icon; your wol.cfg line "attackTypeIcons AbstractCavalry 38214 ..." adds yours, and our DLL did not have
   that key yet. It has it now, with the same wol.cfg line, so the Escopetero shows 1.5x, 1.25x, 0.75x with either DLL.

4. "How are the attack stats displayed on the tooltip?"
   Pictures, pointer resting on an attack row, Escopetero, Koygua (Villero) and Menelik II:
   - your minimized panel today (your uhc.dll):  final\minUIStats-v5-wolmin-tip-escopetero.jpg, -villero, -explorer
   - your big panel today:                         final\minUIStats-v5-wolbig-tip-escopetero.jpg, -villero, -explorer
   - version 5:                                    final\minUIStats-v5-tip-escopetero-ranged.jpg, -tip-escopetero-hand,
                                                   -tip-villero-ranged, -tip-villero-right, -tip-explorer-ranged,
                                                   -tip-explorer-sharpshooter
   The game's tooltip is already worded: the row's name, then "11 (Ranged) Damage", "14 Range with 1 Area", and one
   line per multiplier, "1.5X Bonus Damage against (icon) (Maneuver Unit)". Version 5 keeps all of it and adds two
   lines after the damage, for what the panel adds to the row:
       Attacks every 3.50 s (stopwatch)
       4 projectiles before the reload (cylinder)
   Those two texts are in minuirows.xml (rofTip, projectilesTip), so they can be translated; [string:ID] works there.
   A row too long for its place, shown short (the explorers' "Sharpshooter ATK 750 4.50 3"), now has the same full
   tooltip instead of a plain copy of the row.
   Two things we fixed on the way:
   - the "Hitpoints" line covered the left half of the first attack row, so that row's tooltip only showed on its
     right half; the line is now one row high (proposed-uiunitselectionmin.xml).
   - at 1024x768 the Escopetero's ranged row (three multipliers) ran past the panel's edge; now the last multipliers
     are left out and the row ends with "(+2)"; the tooltip lists them all (final\minUIStats-v5-r1024-escopetero.jpg
     and -r1024-tip-escopetero.jpg). At 1920x1080 every row fits.
   Your big panel, for comparison, shows only the first attack: its 5 rows are Hitpoints, Speed, Resists and one
   attack (minUIStats-v5-wolbig-tip-villero.jpg shows the Villero's Siege ATK only).

Everything else is as in version 4. The pictures and the full test table are in final\README.txt.
