"NEW" Levant: what we found (2026-11-21)
========================================

Short answer
------------
We could not make Levant close the game. We did find one real fault in the map, and it is the best
lead we have: with 6 or 8 players in two equal teams, Levant sometimes cannot place one player's
Town Center. That player then starts with nothing at all (no Town Center, no settlers, no explorer).
In our tests the game went on without crashing, but this is the kind of broken start that can end
badly on other machines or in multiplayer, and it explains reports like "one player had nothing".
A fixed RM4\NEWLevant.xs is in levant\RM4\ (the change is shown in levant\NEWLevant.diff), and a rule
for future maps is at the end.

Everything below was run with WoL's own uhc.dll (the one players have), WoL 1.0.15c, the AI files of
the Steam install, English text, single player only. NEWLevant.xs is the same file in the Steam
install and in every test copy.


1. The fault: a player can start with nothing
---------------------------------------------
How often (the map generated 175 times in the scenario editor, 2 teams, seeds 1 to 25):

  players    2    3    4    5    6    7    8
  no start   0    0    0    0    4    0    3     (out of 25 each)

  6 players: seeds 5 (player 3), 9 (player 5), 17 (player 3), 25 (player 6)
  8 players: seeds 13 (player 3), 14 (player 7), 23 (player 4)

The player gets only the home city water flag. In a skirmish we forced the same thing (see 3):
an AI with no start stays in the game with nothing; a human with no start sees "You abandon your
colony" at once.

Why. The Town Center is placed in one try with four constraints at the same time (NEWLevant.xs,
lines 536-555 and 635-647):
  - within TCfloat metres of the player's spot (100 m with 6+ players, 70 m with 3-5, 15 m with 2);
  - inside the circle playerEdgeConstraint (40 % of the map from the centre), while the team lines
    of rmPlacePlayersLine start at the very edge of the map (0.50,1.00 and 0.00,0.50);
  - 60 m from any other Town Center (avoidTownCenterFar, used when the teams are equal: 3v3, 4v4);
  - 25 m from water or cliffs (longAvoidImpassableLand), with the bay in the north-west.
On some seeds no point satisfies all four, and rmPlaceObjectDefAtLoc places nothing. Nothing checks
it: the next line asks rmGetUnitPosition(rmGetUnitPlacedOfPlayer(TCID, i)) of a unit that does not
exist, so the starting units, mine, trees, herds and nugget of that player are placed around an
invalid point, and fail too.

Checked on those 7 seeds: without the edge circle, every player got a Town Center; without the 60 m
(28 m instead), too; without the 25 m from water, 6 of 7 (8 players, seed 23 still failed). So it is
the combination, not one bad constraint. An unchanged copy of the map under another name fails on
the same seeds, so these comparisons are fair.

The fix (tested). After the normal try, if the player has no Town Center, try again with fewer
constraints (150 m, 28 m from other Town Centers, 10 m from water), and as a last resort with none,
so every player always gets a start. Then use the Town Center that was placed. Result over 100
generations (2, 4, 6 and 8 players, seeds 1 to 25): every player had a Town Center in all 100,
including the 7 seeds above. Maps where the first try works are unchanged, because the fallbacks
only run when it fails. The same change applies as it is to our King of the Hill version of Levant
(maps-koth\RM4\NEWLevant.xs).

3. Forced "no start" in a real skirmish
---------------------------------------
To see what such a game does, we used a copy of Levant where one player's Town Center is never
placed (exactly what happens when the placement fails):
  - 2v2, the AI teammate (player 2) with no start, 10 minutes of game time: no crash, no script
    error. The AI stays in the game with no units.
  - 2v2, the human (player 1) with no start, 3 minutes: "You abandon your colony" at the start; the
    AIs play on; no crash.


4. What was ruled out
---------------------
  - The map script itself: it compiles and runs with 2 to 8 players, teams and free for all, in the
    editor and in skirmishes (no XS error).
  - Long games: 10 minutes of game time per skirmish with 2 to 8 players.
  - King of the Hill rules, and saving and loading a game (see 2).

Not tested: a skirmish on one of the 7 failing seeds (we did not check that the setup screen keeps
a given seed), playing on after a load, our King of the Hill version in a skirmish, games longer
than 10 minutes, Hard or Expert AIs, AIs with accented names (the known trigger bug), a human civ
other than Dutch, Spanish text, and multiplayer.
  - European, Asian, African, Middle Eastern and native AIs, and the Maori AI (its own script).
  - The cliff type "Levant", the sea "Maltese Coast", the lighting, the natives (Bhakti, Sufi):
    all exist in the data and load.
  - The accent bug: players with an accented name (Benito Juárez, Napoleón III ...) break the
    map triggers on 193 maps, Levant too (see README.txt in this folder). That is not a crash.

6. A rule for future maps
-------------------------
Never use a placed object without checking that it was placed. For the Town Center (and anything
else a player must have):
  - after rmPlaceObjectDefAtLoc(TCID, i, ...), read id = rmGetUnitPlacedOfPlayer(TCID, i);
  - if id < 0, place a second object definition with looser constraints (more distance, a shorter
    distance to other Town Centers, less distance to water), and if that also fails, one with no
    constraints at all;
  - only then compute the starting position from the Town Center that exists.
Also: a player spot at the edge of the map plus an edge constraint that keeps the Town Center 10 %
of the map inside is a contradiction that only the max distance can solve; keep spots inside the
allowed circle, or the max distance large enough for the bigger player counts.
