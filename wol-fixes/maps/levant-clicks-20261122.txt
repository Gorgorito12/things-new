"NEW" Levant: clicking everything, 6 and 8 players (2026-11-22)
================================================================

JTMaston (2026-11-22): "try to make a player or all players click on anything clickable on the map; some maps
used to crash after or when clicking on trade units or guardians; do it with 6+ players".

Short answer
------------
We made the game crash: 3 times in 6 games on "NEW" Levant with 6 and 8 players, while one player clicked every
kind of unit (trade caravans, trade route sockets, guardians, treasures, native villages, animals, buildings, other
players' units), after 9 to 13 minutes and 170 to 250 clicks. The two other maps tested the same way ("NEW" Iranian
Plateau and "NEW" Ferghana, 6 players) did not crash, nor did 3 of the Levant games (6 players in teams, 8 players
free for all, and a second 6-player free for all).

The crashes are in Granny, the game's library for 3D models and animations, which is handed model or animation
data that is no longer valid:
  - twice at age3y.exe+0xC07C, while a model is drawn: the game asks Granny for the model's skeleton and gets
    nothing (0) or a garbage address;
  - once inside granny2.dll+0x3A02A, called by the game's unit update (age3y.exe 0x4B6F1F,
    GrannyFindTrackGroupForModel): looking a model up in an animation that points to freed memory.
So the same kind of fault shows at two places: a model or an animation that the game had loaded is gone (freed, or
replaced in memory) while units still use it. We have not yet found which model or animation, nor why it goes; see
"The crash in detail".

This fits what your players report: it happens in big games, after some minutes, around the moment of a click. In
our tests a click moves the camera to a unit somewhere on the map and selects it, so new models and animations get
loaded all the time. The last units clicked before each crash: a Gaia unit near the native villages (crash 1), a
player's JavaneseLabourer with player 1's villagers ordered onto it (crash 2), and the Sufi village temple
(ypNativeTempleSufi, the third Sufi temple clicked in a row; crash 3). Two of the three are next to the Bhakti and
Sufi villages, but this is not proven to be the cause.

What this means for you
-----------------------
- It is not the map script, the AI or the trigger code: the map generates and plays, no XS error was logged; the
  game falls inside the model and animation code.
- We did not see it in the 11 games of yesterday (no clicking, the camera at home), which fits.
- It is not our own test DLL's display statistics: crash 3 happened with them switched off.
- Levant is the map where it showed; with only 2 games on each other map that is not proof the other maps are
  safe.
- Next step on our side: our crash watch now writes the name of the model when the drawing crash happens again, and
  the click tool writes the unit at hand before each click; with the name, the model file can be checked with the
  game's own Granny library (aoe3-blender-tools' gr2_check.py). If you have a crash log (Age3Log.txt) or a dump from
  a player, the model or animation named last in it would help.

The crash in detail (for whoever can debug it)
----------------------------------------------
  EIP 0x40C07C (age3y.exe+0xC07C), access violation (read); the same address in every crash.
  Crashes 1 and 2: EIP 0x40C07C (age3y.exe+0xC07C), access violation (read). The function (0x40C022) takes the
  visual's entry in the game's model cache (global 0xC67318, entry index at [visual+18h]), then calls
  GrannyGetSourceSkeleton([visual+24h], the model instance) and reads the skeleton's bone count at
  [skeleton+4]. The call returned 0 (crash 1: read of address 4) or a garbage pointer (crash 2: read of
  0xC05529A7): the model instance points at model data that is gone. The stack holds the per-model draw
  (0x44EA4B) and the frame's draw (0x451446).
  Crash 3: EIP granny2.dll+0x3A02A, read of 0xA103CF84, in GrannyFindTrackGroupForModel called at 0x4B6F1F with
  the animation of a cache entry ([entry+8], its track groups at +50h) and the model's name: the animation data is
  gone. The stack holds the world update (0x481E07, 0x482049).
  Crash 1: 6 players free for all, Hard, about 10.8 minutes, after 200 clicks; the last unit looked at was a Gaia
  unit (ID 254, kind not recorded by that version of the tool), next in line after the Bhakti village's props.
  Crash 2: 8 players in two teams, Expert, about 9.5 minutes, after 172 clicks; it fell just after player 1's
  villagers were ordered onto a JavaneseLabourer of its own.
  Crash 3: 6 players free for all, Hard, about 13 minutes, after 254 clicks; the last unit was a Sufi village
  temple (ypNativeTempleSufi), the third clicked in a row.
  Read with REA (evidence in our repository, rea/evidence/levant-clicks-20261122/): the cache's loader frees a
  Granny file only on its own error path (0x46E8D4) and frees its vertex section after upload (0x46D297); who
  frees or replaces the data these units still use was not found. [Inference] a model or animation is unloaded
  or reloaded while visuals keep pointers into it; loading many models (a revealed 6-8 player map, the camera
  jumping) makes it more likely. Not established: which file, and whether memory runs short at that moment.
