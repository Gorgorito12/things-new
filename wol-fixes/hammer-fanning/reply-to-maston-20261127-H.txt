Hammer fanning (your request of 2026-11-25): what we built, and three questions

What we found first
- Today each of the two explorer abilities fires ONE shot per use. The borrowed outlaw clip shows three muzzle
  flashes, but in an ability's attack every Attack tag asks the game whether the ability is ready, and the first shot
  starts its recharge, so the second and third tags fire nothing. We traced it on a 5000 HP guardian: 750 damage per
  use, not 2250. <numberprojectiles>3 in explorer.tactics is not read by the game.
- The machine guns' retarget did not work for abilities either: the game would not let an ability's attack take a
  guardian as its next target, because the tactic would give a guardian the explorer's normal shot.

What we built (pack wol-fixes/hammer-fanning, apply_fix.py with --check and --restore)
- A new animation for RangedAttackSharpshoot, for the Explorer only: the pistol at the hip, held by its grip, and the
  left palm sweeping back over the hammer at each of six shots, then back over the top for the next one. The shots are
  0.28 s apart (the borrowed clip's flashes were 0.21 s apart). The model has no hammer and the hand no fingers, so
  the sweep is read in the wrist and forearm, close over the pistol.
- A SharpshootIdle animation (explorer.tactics names it, but it was never defined): the pistol at the hip, the left
  hand ready.
- The outlaw clip is no longer used by the Explorer's RangedAttackSharpshoot; the file stays in the game for anything
  else that uses it. The reload animation is unchanged.
- A new DLL key, powerShots, and the tag <PowerShots>N</PowerShots> on an attack: an ability's attack fires at each
  Attack tag of its animation, up to N shots per use. The ability still has to be ready for the first shot, and its
  recharge still starts with it.
- retargetInAction now works for abilities: when the target dies, the next shot goes to the nearest unit in range that
  the attack has a <rate type> for (Guardian for Hammer Fanning, LogicalTypeValidSharpshoot for Slip Hammering).
- The pack puts <RetargetInAction>1 and <PowerShots>6 on both of the Explorer's ProtoActions (SharpshooterAttack and
  CrackshotAttack), not in explorer.tactics, which ten other units share. It adds the two keys to wol.cfg:
      retargetInAction
      powerShots

Tested in game (single player)
- British Explorer, Hammer Fanning on six outlaw guardians (270 HP): six shots, six guardians dead in one use.
- Slip Hammering on four regulars (200 HP): four shots, four dead; the last two shots found nobody in range.
- Danish Explorer (a different model with the same clips): the animation plays the same way.
- Without the pack: one guardian per use, as today.
- The AI still compiles and plays; no WoL AI script casts these abilities.
- Not tested: multiplayer.

Questions
1. Balance. Every shot now does the full damage: 6 x 750 = 4500 per use of Hammer Fanning, and 6 x 1000 = 6000 for
   Slip Hammering, spread over up to six targets. Should we lower the damage per shot (for example 750 / 6 = 125 to
   keep today's total), or raise the cooldowns (60 s and 90 s today)?
2. The same animation for both abilities? Slip Hammering could get a different gesture (for example one-handed
   rapid fire) if you want them to look different.
3. Retarget on Slip Hammering too, or only on Hammer Fanning against guardians? Today the pack turns it on for both.
