Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-25: the range rings in the options menu
==========================================================================

Tested in single player in a copy of WoL.


Range rings on/off in the options menu
--------------------------------------
Players can now turn the range rings (displayedRange) on or off themselves, in the game's options:

    Options -> UI Options -> Game UI Options -> "Show Range Rings"

It is a check box like the other rows of that page, right under "ALT key shows hitpoint bars", with the tooltip
"Shows how far the selected units and buildings shoot, as rings on the ground, and the range of a building while you
place it. Takes effect at once."

The change shows as soon as the player clicks OK, with no restart, also during a match (Menu -> Options): the rings
of the selected units and of a building being placed appear or disappear as the game resumes. The choice is kept for
the next starts (in uhc-options.cfg next to the log, not in the profile). Until a player chooses, the rings are on.

To install it: the pack wol-fixes/options-menu (python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>"; --check and --restore too). It
adds the field to data\options.xml and two lines to wol.cfg:

    menuOptions displayedRange
    displayedRange

A displayedRange line you already have keeps its arguments (noPlacement, log). The label and tooltip are plain text
in options.xml, so you can change them or use string IDs. If you add the field by hand, keep the file's Windows line
ends: a field with Unix line ends in options.xml is silently left off the screen.

Tested: on and off from the main menu and during a match, both ways (a Fort's rings, and an Outpost's rings while it
was being placed); the choice kept over a restart; noPlacement still honoured; the existing menu switches
(bufferedFileReads, fastVertexFill, fastLastInLine) unchanged beside it; without the menuOptions line the menu is the
stock one.

Videos (GIF):
- docs/media/displayedRange-menu-wol.gif: a selected Fort with no rings; Options, the box ticked, OK: the 33 m ring
  and the dashed 47 m anti-ship ring appear; Options again, unticked, OK: they are gone.
- docs/media/displayedRange-menu-wol-control.gif: WoL today, the same page without the row, no rings.
Picture: docs/media/displayedRange-menu-wol.png, the UI Options page with the new row and its tooltip.

A side note: in single player, every row of the options screen can be changed during a match; nothing there is
greyed out.
