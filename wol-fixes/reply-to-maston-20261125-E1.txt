"A search bar for ... triggers" (#82, third part)
--------------------------------------------------
The trigger editor's list of triggers now has its own "Search:" field, above "Group:". Type part of a trigger's name
and the list keeps only the triggers of that group whose name holds every word (upper or lower case alike). The
trigger you are editing always stays in the list, so the panels on the right never edit a trigger the list hides.

Before building it we checked in the exe how the game knows which trigger a row is: each row carries its trigger's
place in the group, and a click reads that, not the row's position. So a trigger picked from a short list is really
that trigger. We tested it on your campaign's First Barbary War (27 triggers in Ungrouped): with "training" the list
keeps the two P2_...TrainingKicker triggers; picking the second one shows its own 4 conditions and 5 effects (in the
full list the second row is Captain02), and a Loop tick set there landed on that trigger and not on its neighbour.
Insert and Del work with a search typed, and the text stays when you change group.

To make room, the Trigger Editor panel on the left now starts as high as the Triggers panel next to it, and its
search row is level with the search of the Conditions / Effects panel.
