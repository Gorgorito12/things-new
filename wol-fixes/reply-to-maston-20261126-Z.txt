Hi JTMaston,

The new zamburak (your request of 2026-11-26): the midfa's cannon on a swivel on the camel's back, limbered and
unlimbered, the artillerist firing it on foot, as in your reference image.

1. What it is (MEZamburak only, for now)
- The camel is the zamburak's, without its lateral swivel gun. On its withers sits the midfa's bronze barrel, cut
  to 2/3 of its length and at 0.6 of its size, on a fork that turns in a socket. The rider is today's, with his
  model and hat by rank.
- One unit with two modes, like the stock field guns:
  - Limber (as trained): it walks, trots and runs with the gun angled down beside the camel's neck. It cannot
    fire.
  - Unlimber (6.67 s): an attack order, or the new unlimber button, does it. The camel lies down and turns its
    neck aside. He swings the gun out over the camel's right side (+90, square to its spine), gets off and stands
    at his station behind the breech.
  - Firing (2.0 s): he touches the vent with the hot red tip of his linstock, a short plain stick.
  - Reloading (3.0 s): he sticks the linstock in the ground and pushes the gun round through its back until the
    muzzle points at him. He loads the charge and the ball by hand, pushes the gun back to its rest, and takes the
    linstock up.
  - Limber (5.5 s): the limber button, or a move order. He swings the gun back, gets on, and the camel rises.
  - Killed unlimbered: the camel slumps and he falls back. Killed limbered: three of today's camel deaths play, on
    a model with the cannon built in.
- Five of today's limbered deaths (death02, and the havoc deaths front, behind, left and run) rolled the camel
  onto the cannon and drove it into the ground, so the limbered unit no longer plays them.
- With the DLL key turretArc (below), the gun turns on its swivel from +30 to +150 toward its target, and he
  reaches the turned vent from his station. A target outside that arc turns the camel until the target sits at +90.

2. A proposal: the ROF
- The pack sets MEZamburak's CannonAttack ROF from 4.0 to 5.0. That is the shot (2.0 s) plus the reload (3.0 s)
  as they are animated.
- If you want to keep 4.0, or want another number, tell me and I'll fit the reload to it.

3. Questions
- Should ypZamburak and ypZamburakMansabdar get the same model and modes? Today only MEZamburak changes.
- The camel's rank skins (Veteran, Guard, Imperial) are not carried over: every rank shows the basic camel, while
  the rider keeps his rank models and hats. Do you want the rank skins on the new camel too?
- Today's rider carried a carbine on his back in some anims. The new anims don't show it. Should it come back?

4. The DLL key, one line in Startup\wol.cfg (the pack does not edit the cfg):
    turretArc MEZamburak art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\zamburak_cannon\zamburak_cannon.aim
- If you already use it for the gatling camel, add MEZamburak and its file to the same line.
- The aim file is version 2: it needs the DLL of 2026-11-26 or later.
- Without the key the unit still works; the camel turns to face each target and the gun stays at +90.
