Every item below was tested in single player in a copy of WoL and filmed with a control where it shows on
screen. The guide sections and the request rows (docs/wol-requests.md) have the details and the videos.


#76 shipmentCounts: age syntax
------------------------------
Done. `age` now takes one digit from 0 to 4, the same numbers as a card's <age> and the XS cAge1..cAge5
(0 = Age I, 4 = Age V), or two digits with no hyphen for a range, first age then last: age='13' is National
to Industrial, age='04' every age. The old forms ('2-4', '5') are now refused with a log line, so please
rewrite existing effects ('2' becomes '1', '2-4' becomes '13').

About the note on uiDeleteSelectedUnit(): that is the command behind the Delete key. Typed into the console it
crashes the stock game, with or without our DLL, because it first reads the in-game interface, which a console
call does not set up. It has nothing to do with shipmentCounts; our test needed a unit removed, so we deleted
it with an AI rule instead.

#69 Paraguayan works: smoke off 1.5 times the on time
-----------------------------------------------------
Done: the works' chimneys now pause 1.5 times as long as they puff. A pause is 14 to 28 s (21 s on average)
and a puff 8 to 20 s (14 s on average), so each chimney smokes about 40 % of the time. A works with two
chimneys has at least one smoking about two thirds of the time, and an age 3 works about four fifths. Only
works_smoke.particle (and its .xmb) changed (dormanttime 21). Measured in game: one chimney over seven minutes
puffed 8-23 s (13.9 s on average) and paused 13-26 s (20.1 s on average). If you want it lighter still, raise
dormanttime; keep dormanttime x (1 - dormanttimevar) at 12 s or more, so a pause always outlasts the smoke.

#80 Ranged gathering with buildings
-----------------------------------
Buildings can gather now, with a new key, buildingGather. The game never started a building's gather: a Gather
action only begins gathering once the unit has walked to its target, and a building never walks, so it waited
for ever. With the key on, a building whose Gather action has the target within range gathers straight into
your stockpile at the action's rate, and drops the order if the target is out of range. Two things in the
data: a Gather action in the building's tactics, and a <CarryCapacity> in its proto for each resource it
gathers (any amount, because the game refuses a gatherer without one). Add
<AutogatherFrom delay="3">AbstractMine</AutogatherFrom> to the action and the building starts on its own when
idle. Tested with a Market on a mine, a berry bush, a tree and coin crates (three in a row), out of range,
save/load, and against the AI.
Questions:
1. A right-click with a building selected still sets its gather point, so the ways to start it are the tag, a
   trigger, or XS. Is that enough, or do you want a command button?
2. A building only gathers what its player can see, and a Market sees little, so a crate 12 m from its centre
   was ignored until something revealed it. Should a gathering building ignore fog of war, or will you raise
   its LOS?
3. A building gathers wood from a standing tree without cutting it. Is that what you want, or should trees be
   excluded?
4. Which buildings and targets do you have in mind? We'd test with your real data.


#87 numberOfShots: no idle between the shots of a burst
-------------------------------------------------------
Thanks, you were right. The "rifle back to idle" came from our previous fix: between quick shots it played the
action's <idleanim>, and for WoL's ranged infantry that is Volley_standing_idle, the rifle at rest. Now a new
action tag <BurstPose> decides what the unit shows between the shots of a burst. hold is the default: the end
of the attack animation is stretched to fill the interval, so the soldier keeps his rifle at the shoulder from
shot to shot, with no idle and no reload. idle and reload keep the two older behaviours for actions where you
want them. Shot timing is unchanged (0.40 / 0.67 s inside a burst, then the ROF). New videos: the Skirmisher
and the Persian Sarbaz.
Question: each shot still plays the very start of the attack animation (about 40 ms at a 0.4 s interval), as
the stock attack does. Is that acceptable, or should we look at skipping the raise for the 2nd and later
shots?

#84 Dismount: hiding the horse
------------------------------
The horse can now be hidden, and you choose that per unit (hide or keep in make_dismount's recipe, or --horse
in the pack). With hide, the horse is there while the rider gets off and back on and is gone while he is on
foot. On foot the rider stands at the unit's centre, on his selection circle and under his HP bar. When he
dismounts he takes a short step from the horse's side to the centre once the horse is gone, and when he mounts
he steps back before the horse reappears, so each transition is 3.5 s. With keep, the horse stays beside him.
Tested in game with the horse hidden: both transitions, standing and walking, volley fire, bayonet, a new torch
attack against buildings on foot, deaths on foot, a game saved and loaded while dismounted, and the AI still
plays. A test-only copy of the Chasseur also heals and builds on foot, and both animations play (the game
always uses the anim names Heal and Build for those actions, whatever the action's own <anim> says). We also
fixed a bug in the pack's script that had copied 7 techs by mistake and broke the AI's compile.
Questions:
1. The horse appears and disappears instantly, like the stock artillery's limber. Fine, or would you like
   something to cover it, such as a dust puff?
2. Should hidden be the default for the Chasseur, and which other units should get the stance, each with keep
   or hide?

#78 Levant: clicking everything with 6+ players
-----------------------------------------------
We did what you asked: one player clicked every kind of unit (trade caravans, trade sockets, guardians,
treasures, native villages, animals, buildings, other players' units) in 6- and 8-player games, with a crash
watcher. We made the game crash 4 times on "NEW" Levant, after 9 to 13 minutes; there was no crash on "NEW"
Iranian Plateau or "NEW" Ferghana. All crashes are in the 3D model and animation code (Granny): a model the
game had loaded is gone while a unit still uses it.
What we found so far: the game keeps its loaded models under a fixed 30 MB budget and, while over it, unloads
the least recently used model every frame. WoL's art keeps it over that budget the whole game (584 unloads in
9.4 minutes in one test), and big games make it worse; the last crash came one second after 8 models were
unloaded. A smaller second source: a model whose part declaration names a bone that doesn't exist is reloaded
while in use (6 times in one game; we have not named that model yet). The likely fix is to raise or remove the
budget (a key, or a 4-byte patch in the exe); we will test it in the next round, together with a run with only
your uhc.dll, and name the model with the bad bone.
Questions:
1. Do your players' crash reports (Event Viewer > Application > "Application Error") show age3y.exe at offset
   0000c07c or 0000cc7b, or granny2.dll at offset 0003a02a?
2. Do the crashes happen in big games (6-8 players) after several minutes, near native villages?
3. Could you send an Age3Log.txt from right after such a crash?
