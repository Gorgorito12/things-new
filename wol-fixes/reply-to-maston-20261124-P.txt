Oil display before the Great War (#103) and global gathering rates (#105): the panel part
==========================================================================================

Oil only after the Great War
----------------------------
Yes. Every variant of the resource panel (default and detailed, European / Asian / Native, big and minimized: 12 in
all) now holds two panels: one without the oil row, used before the Great War, where the other rows spread over the
freed space, and today's, with oil, used after it. A new DLL option swaps them when the player gets GreatWarizeALL
(every Great War path gives it). With the option off, the panel is exactly today's.

It is an optional pack (wol-fixes\oil-panel): it adds five textures (the backgrounds without the oil row, beside
yours) and replaces the SettlerCountDialog block of data\uimainnew.xml; --restore puts everything back. It does not
need faith; if you take the faith row too, --faith puts it in both panels.

The picture p-oil-panel-default.jpg shows each default variant as the game draws it at 1920x1080: yours today,
before GW, after GW, and the same two with the faith row. p-oil-panel-advanced.jpg shows the detailed variants.
On the way, the after-GW panels keep two fixes of the earlier panel work: the Asian minimized panel shows the oil
amount (the population sat on top of it), and the Native minimized panel shows its icons.

Questions:
1. In the taller rows before the Great War the icons grow a little (at most 10 %) and the rest is wood, so they
   do not reach the numbers. Do you prefer that, or icons that fill the taller rows?
2. Observer mode uses its own layout files, without oil already; we left them alone.

Global gathering rates
----------------------
p-rates-options.jpg shows three places for the rate ("+4.2": the player's income of that resource per second,
from gathering, auto-gathering and trickles, averaged over a few seconds), on your current panels:
  A. on the same line as the amount, right-aligned against the panel's edge (a column of rates);
  B. under the amount, in smaller numbers (tight in the 7-row Asian and Native panels);
  C. only in the row's tooltip ("Income: +4.2 per second").
Which one do you want? A keeps every row readable at a glance; C changes nothing on screen. The colour (green in
the pictures) and per second or per minute are open too. The lag question is answered separately, with
measurements in an 8-player game.
