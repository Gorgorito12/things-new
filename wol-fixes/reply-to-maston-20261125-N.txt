Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-25: "Nursery mechanic" (#107), a first version and our questions
==================================================================================================

A first version is built: new key nurseryFactor in our DLL. Where your request left a choice open we took the
simplest reading (listed after the questions); your answers to the questions at the end may change those points.
Tested in single player in a copy of WoL, with a Bank given the test tags (0.5 gold per mink, max 10, one more every
5 s). GIFs: nurseryFactor-wol (the minks grow, a tech adds 3, the max goes to 15, the panel and its rollover),
nurseryFactor-wol-control (key off), nurseryFactor-ship-wol (the shipment).


How it works
------------
The trickle is the nursery's own AutoGather action, exactly as today (for the mink nursery: 0.5 gold in its tactics),
and our DLL multiplies its rate by the building's factor. So:
- to the owner: a plain AutoGather;
- into the building's own inventory: the same action with the stock tactics tag
  <AddResourcesToInventory>1</AddResourcesToInventory>, which the game already supports;
- techs that change the AutoGather rate keep working as they do now;
- the unit panel's rate row shows the rate already multiplied (0 minks: 0 gold/s; 10 minks: 5 gold/s);
- a trickle to the owner also shows in the global income rates (incomeRates), with no extra work.

The proto block (only the first tag is required):

    <NurseryFactorMax>10</NurseryFactorMax>              the cap
    <NurseryFactorInitial>0</NurseryFactorInitial>       the value of a new building (default 0)
    <NurseryFactorInterval>30</NurseryFactorInterval>    game seconds per growth step (none: no growth with time)
    <NurseryFactorStep>1</NurseryFactorStep>             the size of a step (default 1)
    <NurseryFactorGrowth>linear</NurseryFactorGrowth>    linear or exponential
    <NurseryFactorName>90001</NurseryFactorName>         string ID or plain text: "minks"
    <NurseryFactorRolloverCount>90002</NurseryFactorRolloverCount>  e.g. "{current} out of {max} minks."
    <NurseryFactorRolloverRate>90003</NurseryFactorRolloverRate>    e.g. "Produces one mink every {seconds} seconds."
    <NurseryFactorTrickle>1</NurseryFactorTrickle>       0: a counter only, the AutoGather is not multiplied

The two rollover strings are yours to write (string table IDs, so they can be translated), with the placeholders
{current}, {max}, {name}, {seconds} and {step}.

Each building keeps its own factor, saved in saved games. It grows only once the building is finished, one step per
interval, and stops at the max. The max, the interval and the step are per player and shared by all his nurseries.

Tech effects (type Data):

    NurseryFactor          ProtoUnit target: adds to the factor (Absolute, may be negative) or sets it (Assign),
                           clamped to 0..max
    NurseryFactorMax       ProtoUnit target: the max
    NurseryFactorInterval  ProtoUnit target: the seconds per step
    NurseryFactorStep      ProtoUnit target: the step (optional)
    FreeHomeCityUnitByNurseryFactor   Player target: the stock FreeHomeCityUnit, amount x the factor

    <Effect type='Data' amount='1.00' subtype='FreeHomeCityUnitByNurseryFactor' unittype='CrateofCoin'
            nursery='MinkNursery' consume='all' relativity='Absolute'>
        <Target type='Player'/></Effect>

consume='all' empties the factor after the shipment; consume='2' takes 2 per nursery and ships for what it took.

The UI: in the unit panel of the minimized interface, a row "Minks 3/10", and on hover your two lines as bullets
("- 3 out of 10 minks." / "- Produces one mink every 5 seconds."). For a single nursery that row takes the place of
the AutoGather rate row; a selection of several still shows the summed rate. The full interface's stat panel is not
done yet. We also add a text {uhcNurseryFactor} ("3/10 minks") you can put in your own layouts.

What we measured:
- two Banks with 9 minks each: 9.06 gold per second (2 x 9 x 0.5 = 9.0 expected); key off: 1.00 per second;
- each Bank counts its own minks; the techs add 3, raise the max and shorten the step as written;
- a saved game keeps each Bank's minks and its timer (the next mink came at the same game time after the load);
- in a skirmish, consume='2' brought 4 crates of coin for two Banks, then consume='all' brought 14 and emptied them;
- 40 Banks with the tags: no measurable cost (the simulation's time per frame 0.1 ms with and without the key);
- into the building's own inventory: a Bank at 10 minks stored 4.96 gold per second in itself (5.0 expected) and the
  player got none. The building needs a <CarryCapacity> for that resource, the most it can hold: without one it
  stores nothing;
- exponential growth (rate 0.5): 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 10 minks.

One thing to know about saved games, which is how the game itself works: when a game loads, the game applies the
tech effects again in the order the techs have in the techtree file, not the order they were researched. A max set
to 4 by one tech and raised by 5 by a tech researched later came back as 4 instead of 9. Effects that only add
(Absolute) come back right; mixing Assign and Absolute on the same value across techs can change after a load.

What the first version does until you answer
---------------------------------------------
1. Exponential: each step adds max(step, factor x <NurseryFactorGrowthRate>), rounded down, so it can leave 0.
2. The shipment counts all the player's units of the nursery type; consume='all' empties all of them.
3. consume='2' takes at most 2 from each nursery; one with 1 mink left gives 1.
4. The units come to the home city gather point, like any FreeHomeCityUnit.
5. A captured nursery keeps its minks; a transform into another nursery unit keeps them, cut to its max; into a unit
   that is not a nursery, they are lost.
6. A factor tech changes all the player's built nurseries of that unit.
7. A lowered max cuts the nurseries above it at once.
8. NurseryFactorMax 0 means a max of 0 (no minks); there is no "no max" yet.
9. The panel shows just "3/10"; the second rollover line shows whenever the nursery grows with time, full or not.
10. Only the selected unit's panel.
11. The owner's trickle shows in the global income; a trickle into the building's inventory does not.
12. Whole numbers.
