Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-25: "Nursery mechanic" (#107), the design and our questions
=============================================================================================

Nothing is built yet. This is how we plan to do it; your answers to the questions at the end decide the open
points before we build the first version.


How it would work
-----------------
The trickle is the nursery's own AutoGather action, exactly as today (for the mink nursery: 0.5 gold in its tactics),
and our DLL multiplies its rate by the building's factor. So:
- to the owner: a plain AutoGather;
- into the building's own inventory: the same action with the stock tactics tag
  <AddResourcesToInventory>1</AddResourcesToInventory>, which the game already supports;
- techs that change the AutoGather rate keep working as they do now;
- the unit panel's rate row shows the rate already multiplied (0 minks: 0 gold/s; 10 minks: 5 gold/s);
- a trickle to the owner also shows in the global income rates (incomeRates), with no extra work.

The proto block (only the first tag is required):

    <NurseryFactorMax>10</NurseryFactorMax>              the cap
    <NurseryFactorInitial>0</NurseryFactorInitial>       the value of a new building (default 0)
    <NurseryFactorInterval>30</NurseryFactorInterval>    game seconds per growth step (none: no growth with time)
    <NurseryFactorStep>1</NurseryFactorStep>             the size of a step (default 1)
    <NurseryFactorGrowth>linear</NurseryFactorGrowth>    linear or exponential
    <NurseryFactorName>90001</NurseryFactorName>         string ID or plain text: "minks"
    <NurseryFactorRolloverCount>90002</NurseryFactorRolloverCount>  e.g. "{current} out of {max} minks."
    <NurseryFactorRolloverRate>90003</NurseryFactorRolloverRate>    e.g. "Produces one mink every {seconds} seconds."
    <NurseryFactorTrickle>1</NurseryFactorTrickle>       0: a counter only, the AutoGather is not multiplied

The two rollover strings are yours to write (string table IDs, so they can be translated), with the placeholders
{current}, {max}, {name}, {seconds} and {step}.

Each building keeps its own factor, saved in saved games. It grows only once the building is finished, one step per
interval, and stops at the max. The max, the interval and the step are per player and shared by all his nurseries.

Tech effects (type Data):

    NurseryFactor          ProtoUnit target: adds to the factor (Absolute, may be negative) or sets it (Assign),
                           clamped to 0..max
    NurseryFactorMax       ProtoUnit target: the max
    NurseryFactorInterval  ProtoUnit target: the seconds per step
    NurseryFactorStep      ProtoUnit target: the step (optional)
    FreeHomeCityUnitByNurseryFactor   Player target: the stock FreeHomeCityUnit, amount x the factor

    <Effect type='Data' amount='1.00' subtype='FreeHomeCityUnitByNurseryFactor' unittype='CrateofCoin'
            nursery='MinkNursery' consume='all' relativity='Absolute'>
        <Target type='Player'/></Effect>

consume='all' empties the factor after the shipment; consume='2' takes 2 per nursery and ships for what it took.

The UI: in the unit panel, a row "Minks 3/10" (both the full and the minimized interface), and on hover your two
lines. We also add a text {uhcNurseryFactor} you can put in your own layouts.


Questions
---------
1. "Exponential": does each step add a share of the current factor (more minks breed faster: 4, 5, 6, 8, 10), does
   the step itself grow (1, 2, 4, 8...), or does the time between steps get shorter? And from 0: should it take one
   first step, or stay at 0 until a tech adds some?
2. The shipment with several nurseries: does it ship for the sum of all the player's nurseries? Does "reset to 0"
   empty all of them?
3. "Decrease 2 by 2": per nursery (each gives 2 minks and ships 2 crates), or 2 in total? A nursery with 1 mink left:
   does it give 1, or nothing?
4. Where should the shipped units appear: at the home city gather point, like every shipment, or at the nursery?
5. A captured nursery: does it keep its minks? A nursery that transforms into another nursery unit: keep them (cut to
   the new max)?
6. A tech that changes the factor: all the player's nurseries of that unit, or only the one that researched it?
7. A tech that lowers the max: cut the nurseries above it at once, or let them keep the extra?
8. Should a nursery with no max be possible?
9. The panel while it grows: just "3/10 minks", or also the time to the next one ("3/10, next in 14s")? When it is
   full, should the second rollover line ("Produces one mink every Z seconds") still show?
10. Should the build button's rollover of the nursery also show the max and the rate, or only the selected
    building's panel?
11. A trickle to the owner shows in the global income rates; a trickle into the building's own inventory does not,
    since it is not in the stockpile yet. Is that right for you?
12. The factor is always a whole number (minks), and a Percent tech rounds down. Right?
