Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-29: the guerrilla mechanic (request #114)
============================================================================

How the game's guerrilla works today (we read it in the game's code, then checked it in game)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- <Partisans> in civs.xml is only on or off. 1 turns it on, any other number turns it off. A text that is not a
  number counts as 1: the Tupinamba's <partisans>UybassyArcher</partisans> just means "on". The unit name there is
  not read.
- A building has one PartisanType and one PartisanCount, the same for every civ. A second <PartisanType> line
  replaces the first. So today the Town Center, the Artillery Depot and the Market, which list UybassyArcher and then
  SerbianInferno, give SerbianInferno to the Tupinamba too. In game, a Serbian Town Center gave 6 SerbianInferno.
- The units appear when a finished building of a civ with <Partisans> dies, inside its footprint, owned by its
  player. A trigger's Kill gives none.

1. Can one building have several guerrillas, one per civ?
   Not in the base game: one unit and one count per building, for every civ. So it is done now, as a new DLL key,
   partisans (one line in wol.cfg: "partisans", or "partisans log" to see each guerrilla in the log). In the
   building's proto:

       <CivPartisan civ="Spanish" count="3">Rodelero</CivPartisan>

   One line per civ. That civ gets this unit and count from this building, even without <Partisans> in civs.xml.
   Without count, the building's PartisanCount is used. A civ with no line keeps PartisanType / PartisanCount, as
   today, so the Serbians and the Tupinamba do not change.

2. Change the unit and the amount by techs: done, as two Data subtypes on the building:

       <Effect type ='Data' amount ='0' subtype ='PartisanType' relativity ='Assign' unittype ='Pikeman'>
           <Target type ='ProtoUnit'>Barracks</Target></Effect>
       <Effect type ='Data' amount ='5' subtype ='PartisanCount' relativity ='Assign'>
           <Target type ='ProtoUnit'>Barracks</Target></Effect>

   For the amount, Assign sets it, Absolute adds to it and Percent multiplies it. Only the player who gets the tech
   changes, and a saved game keeps it. The new per-civ effect attribute (onlyciv, sent in the same round) should work
   on them too; that pair is not tested yet.

3. Spawn from the nearby buildings: done, as a tag on the destroyed building:

       <PartisanFromNearby>30</PartisanFromNearby>

   The number is the radius in metres. The unit and the count are the destroyed building's, so the total stays the
   same. The units come out beside the owner's finished buildings within 30 m, one at a time to each, the closest
   first. A foundation does not count. With no building in range, they come out of the destroyed building, as today.

Tested in game (skirmishes, the Spanish against the Serbian AI): a Spanish Barracks gave its 3 Rodeleros while a Serbian
Barracks still gave its 2 SerbianInferno; a tech turned the Spanish Barracks' guerrilla into 5 Pikemen, also after a
save and load; a Spanish Stable's 4 Rodeleros came out beside two Houses and an Outpost; it also works beside WoL's own
DLL, and the AI still runs.

GIFs: partisans-wol (the Spanish Barracks and Stable fall: Rodeleros from the Barracks, then beside the nearby
buildings) and partisans-wol-control (the same without the key: nothing from the Spanish buildings).

The pack wol-fixes/guerrilla/ gives the Spanish a guerrilla of their own as an example (Rodelero: Town Center 4,
Barracks 2, Stable 2; --unit picks another unit, --nearby 30 turns on the nearby mode), with --check and --restore.

Questions, so we can finish it the way you want (until you answer, the build does what is written after "Now:"):
1. From nearby buildings: should the destroyed building's guerrilla be split among them (the same total), or should
   each nearby building spawn its own guerrilla?
   Now: split, the same total, closest building first.
2. Which buildings count as "nearby": any of the owner's buildings, or only the ones that have a guerrilla themselves?
   Now: any finished building of the owner.
3. After they appear, the new units walk over to the nearest group of the same unit and stand in a line. Is that fine,
   or should they stay where they appear?
   Now: they walk. We did not find what makes them walk, so we left it as it is.
4. The Town Center, Artillery Depot and Market give SerbianInferno to the Tupinamba today (see above). Should the
   Tupinamba get UybassyArcher there, as a <CivPartisan civ="Tupinamba"> line?
   Now: unchanged.
5. Which buildings, units and counts do you want for the Spanish? The pack only uses Rodelero as an example.
