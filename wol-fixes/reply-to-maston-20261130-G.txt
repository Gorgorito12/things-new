Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-30: the guerrilla, nearby mode v2 (request #114)
==================================================================================

Thanks for the answers of 2026-11-27. This is what changed, one point per answer:

1. "Each nearby building spawns its own; the radius is global for all the player's buildings (except walls), set and
   enabled on the civ in civs.xml."
   Done. The radius is now a civ tag in civs.xml, inside the <civ>:

       <PartisanNearby>30</PartisanNearby>

   The number is the radius in metres (above 0, up to 200). With it, the destruction of ANY finished building of that
   civ's player (with a guerrilla or not; walls excluded) makes every building of that player within 30 m that has a
   guerrilla spawn its own unit and count beside itself. The base game's rules stay: a trigger's Kill and the owner's
   delete set off nothing. Walls never spawn and never set it off.
   The building tag <PartisanFromNearby> of the last version is gone: the DLL no longer reads it. If it is still in
   the data, the log says "<PartisanFromNearby> is no longer used, see <PartisanNearby> in civs.xml" and nothing else
   happens.

2. "Only those with guerrilla, because the amounts spawned have to correspond to the partisan assigned."
   Done. A nearby building counts only if it has a guerrilla for that civ: its <CivPartisan civ="..."> line, or
   PartisanType/PartisanCount when the civ has <Partisans>1</Partisans>. Each one gives its own unit and its own
   count, with the player's techs applied (a tech that changes a Barracks' unit and count changes what that Barracks
   gives as a nearby building too). Buildings without a guerrilla, foundations and walls give nothing.

3. "They should walk to the building's gather point if spawned from a nearby destruction, otherwise normal spawn."
   Done. The units of each nearby building get the same order the game gives to the units that building trains (we
   call the game's own function for it): they walk to its gather point, as a group. If the gather point is on a unit,
   they get the order on that unit; on the building itself, they go inside. A building with no gather point (one that
   cannot have one, such as the Market, or one that was never given one): they stay beside it.
   When the destroyed building itself spawns (see question 1 below), it is the normal spawn, with no order.

4. "Yes, SerbianInferno is deprecated at the moment" (UybassyArcher back for the Tupinamba).
   Done in the pack: <CivPartisan civ="Tupinamba">UybassyArcher</CivPartisan> on TownCenter, ArtilleryDepot and
   Market, with each building's own PartisanCount (6, 3 and 1). We checked it with the pack's --check, not in a
   game, because we do not test with the Tupinamba; the mechanism is the one we tested with the Spanish.

5. "That's on me" (Spanish buildings, units and counts).
   Understood: the pack stays an example (Rodelero: Town Center 4, Barracks 2, Stable 2).

The pack wol-fixes/guerrilla/:
    python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>" --nearby 30     the Spanish lines, <PartisanNearby>30</PartisanNearby> for the
                                                       Spanish in civs.xml, the Tupinamba lines, `partisans` in the cfg
    python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>" --check         what is applied
    python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>" --restore       everything back as it was (protoy.xml, civs.xml, the cfg)

Tested in game (Spanish against the Serbian AI):
- A House (no guerrilla) destroyed next to a Barracks, a Stable and a Market (test data: 3 Rodeleros on the Market):
  2 Rodeleros came out beside the Barracks and 2 beside the Stable and walked to their gather points; the Market's 3
  stayed beside it (a Market has no gather point). Two more Houses, a foundation, a wall and a Barracks 50 m away gave
  nothing; the House gave nothing.
- A wall destroyed: nothing. A Stable with no building with a guerrilla in range: its own 2 Rodeleros, as today.
- A tech that sets the Barracks' guerrilla to 5 Pikemen, then a save and a load: a House destroyed next to the
  Barracks gave 5 Pikemen.
- The Serbians (no tag): as before, their Barracks gave 2 SerbianInferno, and the Stable next to it nothing.
- Also beside WoL's own uhc.dll; the AI kept playing; no measurable cost.

One thing you may see: after the move, idle units of the same type that stand close to each other may come together
(in 3 of 9 runs the Stable's pair, about 20 m from the Market's three, joined them a few seconds after arriving). That
is the game's own behaviour, the same with the key off.

GIFs: partisans-wol (a House falls; the Barracks' and the Stable's Rodeleros walk to their gather points, the blue
banners; the Market's stay) and partisans-wol-control (the same scene without the key: nothing).

   Now: it spawns its own, as in the normal game (only if it has one).
2. Every destruction sets off all the buildings with a guerrilla in range again, with no limit: losing five houses
   next to a Barracks gives that Barracks' guerrilla five times. Do you want a cap, or a cooldown per building?
   Now: no limit.
3. The Serbians keep SerbianInferno (<partisans>1</partisans> and the last PartisanType of those buildings) until you
   remove it from the data; the pack does not touch them. Is that what you want for now?
