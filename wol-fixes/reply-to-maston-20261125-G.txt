=== Select the fattened livestock (request #106, from a player in Discord) ===

A player asked for a button that selects only the livestock that is fully fattened, since the Middle East civs and
the Spanish immigrants make livestock more important.

New DLL key: selectFattened. A square button above the resource panel, at its left end, shows how many of your
herdables (sheep, cows, goats, llamas, camels, hens...) have reached their maximum food, and selects all of them when
clicked, on the whole map; past the selection limit (75 in WoL), the ones nearest the centre of the screen. It hides
when there are none. Shift+Ctrl+F does the same ("Select Fattened Livestock" in the hotkey menu). It only changes
your own selection, like a click: nothing for the simulation or the other players. It uses the game's own selection
calls, the ones its "find all of a type" hotkeys use.

The pack wol-fixes/select-fattened adds the button to data\uimainnew.xml (outside the resource panel's block, so the
oil and faith panel packs are not affected), the hotkey to data\DefaultKeyMapY.xml, two strings (English and
Spanish), the icon, and the cfg line.
