=== Select the fattened livestock (request #106, from a player in Discord) ===

A player asked for a button that selects only the livestock that is fully fattened, since the Middle East civs and
the Spanish immigrants make livestock more important.

New DLL key: selectFattened. A small square button sits above the resource panel's left end, right of the home city
flag. It shows how many of your herdables (sheep, cows, goats, llamas, camels, hens...) have reached their maximum
food, and clicking it selects all of them, on the whole map; past the selection limit (75 in WoL), the ones nearest
the centre of the screen. It hides when there are none. Shift+Ctrl+F does the same ("Select Fattened Livestock" in
the hotkey menu, group "Find Unit Hotkeys"). The tooltip reads "Fattened livestock: 3. Click to select them
(Shift+Ctrl+F)." It only changes your own selection, like a click: nothing for the simulation or the other players.
It uses the game's own selection calls, the ones its "find all of a type" hotkeys use, and costs about 20
microseconds ten times a second with 250 units.

The pack wol-fixes/select-fattened adds the button to data\uimainnew.xml (outside the resource panel's block, so the
oil and faith panel packs still apply; with both, restore the oil pack first), the hotkey to data\DefaultKeyMapY.xml,
two strings (690911, 690912; English and Spanish), the icon art\ui\ingame\uhc_select_fattened.ddt, and the cfg line.

Seen in game (single player, Persians, full interface, with the oil panel pack too): the button shows 3, a click
selects the three fattened goats, villagers butcher them and the button hides at 0, then three hens fatten at a pen
and it comes back. Also checked with WallMapu (minimized interface) and Ethiopians.
