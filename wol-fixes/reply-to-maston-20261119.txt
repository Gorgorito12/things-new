   the game's worded tooltip and adds "Attacks every 3.50 s" and "4 projectiles before the reload".

Wanders and scouts (request #68)
--------------------------------
New DLL key autoScout and the pack auto-scout\ (README.txt there):
- Any unit can get DE's envoy-style auto scout from data alone: a tactic named AutoScout in its tactics file and two
  buttons (Start scouting / Stop scouting). It goes to the nearest unexplored ground it can reach (land, water or
  air), then the next; once everything is explored, it revisits the ground seen longest ago. Any move, attack,
  gather, garrison or Stop order from its owner switches it off, as in DE.
- The raven: it did not circle because of the animal wander (it belongs to no herd). The game walks every idle unit
  back to its last ordered spot when it strays (a "leash"); the raven drifted and was walked back, over and over.
  With the key its moves are exempt and its leash point follows it.
- The pack makes the raven, both pet parrots and the French balloon scout from birth (with the two buttons), removes
  the raven's WanderDistance, and gives the Dutch envoy the buttons (off at birth). Which units get it is your call;
  the README shows how to add it to any unit.
- Great Lakes, two ravens, share of the map explored: today 9.4 % at 3 min and 9.7 % at 6 min; with auto scout
  63.7 % and 100 %. Video: docs\media\autoScout-wol.mp4 (with the key) and autoScout-wol-control.mp4 (without).
- Tested in single player, also beside your uhc.dll; not in multiplayer. The button tooltips are in English only
  (string IDs 690901 and 690902).
