every 8 s, the default max of 10). GIFs: nurseryFactor-wol (growth, the progress pie, goats sent in),

What changed, answer by answer
------------------------------
1. Exponential: step t (t = 0, 1, 2...) adds b^t, with b set in the proto:

       <NurseryFactorGrowth>exponential</NurseryFactorGrowth>
       <NurseryFactorGrowthBase>2</NurseryFactorGrowthBase>     (default 2)

   With b = 2 the Bank went 1, 3, 7, then 10 (15 cut at the max). The factor stays a whole number; the fraction of a
   step (b = 1.5 gives 1, 1.5, 2.25...) is carried to the next one, so b = 1.5 adds 1, 1, 2, 4, 5... A tech can change
   b too: subtype NurseryFactorGrowthBase. The old NurseryFactorGrowthRate tag is gone.
2. As before: the shipment sums all the player's nurseries, and consume='all' empties them all.
3. and 4. The command buttons (below): units bought with the factor appear at the nursery; units from a card still at
   the home city gather point.
5. A captured nursery keeps its factor (and a transform into another nursery keeps it too, no longer cut).
6. As before: a tech hits all the player's nurseries of that unit.
7. A lowered max no longer cuts anything. A nursery above it keeps the extra until it spends it; it just does not grow,
   and nothing adds to it, while it is above. Tested: 10 minks, max lowered to 5, the Bank kept 10; a -2 tech left it
   at 8 of 5.
8. NurseryFactorMax is optional now, default 10. Any NurseryFactor tag makes the unit a nursery.
9. The panel shows just "3/10 minks" (your NurseryFactorName), in both the minimized and the full interface, and while
   the nursery grows the portrait shows the train button's blue pie filling up toward the next step (agent P's shared
   pie). At the max, no pie. The rollover still has your two lines.
10. to 12. As before.


The garrison tag
----------------
    <NurseryFactorGarrisonUnit amount="3">ypGoat</NurseryFactorGarrisonUnit>

A unit of that unit or unit type that enters the nursery is removed at once (no death, no corpse) and adds the amount.
You can put several of these tags; the first that matches is used. The nursery also needs the normal garrison tags
for that unit (<MaxContained> and a <Contain>). In the test, two goats sent into the Bank were taken: 3 -> 6 -> 9 minks.


The command buttons
-------------------
    <NurseryFactorCommand factor="-4" unit="CrateofCoin" count="2">SellMinks</NurseryFactorCommand>
    <NurseryFactorCommand factor="3" cost="Wood 100, Food 50">BuyMinks</NurseryFactorCommand>

Each is a button, like the market's buy and sell buttons. factor is what it adds (negative: what it spends), cost is
what it costs (resource names as in the techtree), unit and count are the units it makes at the nursery. Any of them
can be left out, so one button can do both (pay resources and spend factor for units, for example).

The button itself is a normal command: its name in data\uhc\customprotounitcommands.xml, an entry in
protounitcommands.xml whose command is nurseryFactorCommand("SellMinks") (with your icon and rollover), and a
<Command> on the nursery. Each selected nursery of the player with that command runs it.

In the test: Sell took 4 minks and put 2 crates of coin beside the Bank (10 -> 6); Buy paid 100 wood and 50 food for 3
(6 -> 9). When no selected nursery can do it, the button is greyed and its rollover says why in red: "2/10 minks: 4
needed", "10/10 minks: full", or "Not enough resources".

The commands travel through the game's own network command, so every player's game runs them the same way and saved
games keep the result. (Single player tested only.)


Questions
---------
1. t, the exponent: we set it back to 0 only when a nursery is emptied (a shipment with consume='all', or a sale down
   to 0). A nursery sold down to 2 keeps its t, so with b = 2 its next step can fill it at once (the test: +8, then
   +16, both cut at 10). Should t go back to 0 on any sale or shipment instead, or follow the factor (the t a nursery
   would have reached at that many minks)?
2. A unit sent into a full nursery: we leave it inside as a normal garrison, instead of taking it for nothing. When
   there is room for only part of its amount (9 of 10, a goat worth 3), it is taken and the factor stops at 10. Right?
3. The units a button makes ignore the population cap, and a button with no room around the nursery for all its units
   does nothing. Should they respect the population cap?
4. The panel: "3/10 minks" as one text (the game's own rows are "Label: value", e.g. "Minks: 3/10"). Is that the look
   you want?
5. The second rollover line ("Produces ... every ... seconds") still shows when the nursery is full. Hide it then?
6. An ally's goat that enters your nursery counts too (today any unit that matches counts, whoever owns it). Should
   only the owner's units count?
