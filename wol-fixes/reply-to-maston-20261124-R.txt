Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-11-24: displayedRange (#92), the Fort's ring and the Grenzer's extra ring
==========================================================================================================

Tested in single player in a copy of WoL and filmed. The guide section "displayedRange" has the numbers and the
videos.


"The fort has 26 range; the normal range ring is too close to the building; same for outposts"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You were right, and there were two causes.

1. The ring files were drawn too small. Our ring models were written in the scale the game's own model files declare
   (0.615 units per metre), but the game ignores that number and reads the vertices as metres. So every range ring
   since the coloured version (2026-11-22) showed only 0.615 of its size: the Fort's "26 m" ring lay about 16 m from
   its centre, and its "40 m" anti-ship ring about 25 m out, close to where the cannon really reaches. That is what
   you saw. We measured it with Settlers placed at known distances, fixed the ring files, and checked again: a 31 m
   ring now passes between markers at 30 and 32 m, a 35 m ring between 34 and 36 m. Please copy the new rings folder
   (art\effects\uhc_rings) over the old one.

2. The range itself is not measured from the centre:

- From a building, MaxRange starts at its walls (its obstruction square). The Fort is 12 x 12 m, so its 26 starts
  6 m from the centre.
- The target's size counts too: the game takes off the target's radius x 1.41. For an infantry unit (radius 0.49)
  that is 0.69 m more.
- A unit that shoots adds its own radius the same way (another 0.69 m for infantry).

We checked it in the exe and then in game: Settlers placed at exact distances from the Fort's walls were shot at
26.08, 26.31 and 26.68 m, and not at 26.83, 26.9 and 27.2 m. So the edge is 26 + 0.69. Their centres were up to
34.4 m from the Fort's centre. The Outpost and the Blockhouse behave the same.

The ring now shows where an infantry unit's centre comes into range:
- Fort: 33 m from the centre (6 + 26 + 0.69), and its anti-ship attack 47 m, dashed.
- Outpost: 27 m (2 + 24 + 0.69), and 43 m dashed.
- Blockhouse and War Hut: 29 m. Town Center: 37 m.
- Grenzer and Skirmisher: 21 m. Crossbowman: 17 m.
The ring sizes now go every metre from 4 to 50 m, so a ring is at most half a metre off.

Two things a round ring cannot show:
- Past the corners of a building the reach goes a little further, because the real zone is the building's square
  grown by the range: 2.5 m more at the Fort's corners, 0.8 m at an Outpost's. Inside the ring is always in range.
- Cavalry are hit 0.4 m further out and cannons 0.7 m, ships more.

One thing about the Fort's data, in case it matters to you: a building only shoots at what you see, and line of
sight is measured from the centre. The Fort's <LOS> is 26, shorter than its 33 m reach. Your age-up techs
Colonialize and Fortressize add 7 + 4 to every building, so a Fort normally sees 37 m and can shoot at its ring
(the videos use those two techs). But a Fort with only its base 26 LOS (no age tech, in our test scenario) shot only
at units about 26 m from its centre, even when they were well in range. If you want a Fort to use its full range on
its own in every case, its LOS would need to be about 33.


"What is the extra ring showing on the grenzer?"
------------------------------------------------
It was the Grenzer's torch: BuildingAttack, 6 m, on LogicalTypeShipsAndBuildings. Crossbowmen, Skirmishers and most
infantry have the same one. It is no longer drawn: an attack that can only hit buildings (and ships) is left out
when the unit also has an attack on units. A unit whose only ranged attack is on buildings (a mortar) still gets
its ring, dashed. A dashed ring now means "this attack cannot hit your army": only ships (the Fort's and the
Outpost's anti-ship attack) or only buildings. If you would rather see the torch ring on infantry too, tell us and
it becomes an option.


"Try to make tests with buildings attacking units while the ring is shown"
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Filmed: a Fort, an Outpost and a Blockhouse, each selected with its rings, with enemy Settlers placed just inside and
just outside the ring (5 HP each in the test data, so one shot kills). Each building shoots the ones inside and
leaves the ones outside. On a diagonal the Fort also hits one 33.9 m out, just past its 33 m ring: that is the corner
effect above.

With units walking in, the first shot comes a little after they cross the ring: in our test, Pikemen walking at a
Fort were about 2 s (and 7 to 10 m) inside the ring when the first cannonball left. Units standing still are shot
exactly by the ring, so we think the attack starts at the ring and the cannonball leaves later in it; we did not
trace that delay.

The earlier displayedRange videos (Alt, placement ghost, enemy and ally) are refilmed with the new rings.
