Reply to JTMaston, 2026-11-21 (feedback on the 2026-11-20 round)
================================================================

Everything below was tested in single player in copies of WoL with our UHC.dll; the min UI and showGatherRate were also
checked beside your own uhc.dll (with our dinput8.dll). Not tested in multiplayer. Each folder has its README in English.

Veterancy: rank-up text off, darker border (#74)
-------------------------------------------------
"The rank up text needs to be disabled."
- New option: veterancyBonus notext. A unit reaching a rank shows no text over it; the mark over the HP bar and the
  unit panel row stay. WoL's line becomes "veterancyBonus notext".
- The marks have a darker black border (12 px wider inward on the traced sheet), so they read better in game. The
  silhouettes and the loop holes did not move. Each mark is still centred on the HP bar (0 px measured on Hussars
  and Crossbowmen, ranks 1-5).
- Video (notext, the new marks): docs\media\veterancyBonus-icons-wol. Folder: wol-fixes\veterancy-marks\.

Special transforms: the toast and the dance (#73)
-------------------------------------------------
"The not enough resources text should show up like the normal toast text."
- Done. When the cost is the only reason, the button stays live (with the red "Not enough resources" line in its
  rollover) and a click shows the game's own message and sound, as for Train: "55 food, 70 gold required to build
  another Askari." Three youths selected give one message, not three. The fireship does the same ("150 wood
  required to build another Pyrpoliko.") and keeps its button. A placement or build limit reason still greys the
  button with that reason.
"Did the dancing animation disappear?"
- Yes, and it is back. The dance lived only in the construction stages of maturing2/3/4.xml; a transform creates the
  maturing object already built, so only the "finished" stage showed (fire and decal, no youth). apply_fix.py now
  gives the finished stage the last dancer stage (Askari keeps the rifle, Escort its heal anim). WOLAfricanUhlanMaturing
  uses maturing4.xml too, so it dances as well.
- But these four maturings produce their unit at once, so the youth now dances BESIDE the new unit for the maturing's
  lifespan (20 s for the Askari, up to 50 s for the Mountain Gun). In the old placed route the youth danced only while
  building (about 4 s) and then left an empty decal.
Questions:
1. Should the youth dance beside the new unit for the whole lifespan, for part of it, or should the unit wait until the
   dance ends? Waiting needs a data change (a delay before the unit appears, or the Maintain action your own button
   maturings use).
2. Important, the AI: the pack replaces the youth's <Train> rows by command buttons, and the AI builds these four
   maturings only through those rows. With the pack, a Zulu AI cannot build them. apply_fix.py --keep-train keeps the
   rows (the AI works, but a human player sees two buttons per maturing). Which do you want? A third way would be a key
   of ours that hides the placing buttons from human players only.

Paraguayan works: smoke on and off (#69)
-----------------------------------------
"Is it possible to make the works' flues alternate at random intervals between puffing and not puffing smoke?"
- Done in works_smoke.particle alone: each chimney puffs for 8-20 s, then pauses for 12-25 s, with a random first wait
  of 0-16 s. The engine rolls new times for each chimney at every puff and pause, so no two chimneys are in step, not
  even the two of one works. A pause is longer than the smoke lives, so the flue really clears.
- Measured in game in the three ages: every chimney stopped and restarted 5-8 times in 4 minutes; each works went fully
  quiet several times, while a row of works was never without smoke for more than a few seconds.
- Each chimney now smokes about 43 % of the time (a two-chimney works about two thirds, an age-3 works about four
  fifths). Tell us if you want it lighter or heavier.
- Seen in the films with both the old and the new file, so not caused by this: two age-1 Food works gave no smoke for
  their first 1-2 minutes of game time, then smoked normally. We did not find why.
- Video: wol-fixes\paraguayan-works\media\paraguayanWorks-smoke-wol (the previous always-on smoke: -steady).
