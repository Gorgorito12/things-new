An update on the gatling camel (#102), after the user's review: the gun now turns on its post, the deployed
unit no longer dismounts, and the reload uses a new magazine.

1. The gun turns, not the camel
- When the target is within 90 degrees of either side of the camel's front, the camel stays still and only the
  Gatling turns on its post toward it; the gunner turns with it, his hands staying on the crank and the breech.
  The shots and the muzzle flash leave from the turned muzzle.
- When the target is beyond that (behind the camel), the camel turns to face it as it does today, and the gun
  comes back to the middle.
- This needs a new DLL key, turretArc, and one line in Startup\wol.cfg:
    turretArc GatlingCamel art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_A55.aim GatlingCamelDeployed art\WoL\units\cavalry_ranged\gatling_camel\gatling_camel_B55.aim
  It reads the game's own <Turret> tag on the attack, which the game parses but never uses:
    <turret tracking="always" maxTurnAnglePerSecond="240">90</turret>
  (90 = the arc each side, 240 = how fast the gun turns on screen, in degrees a second). Both gatling camel
  tactics in the pack already carry it. Without the key, the units work as before, with the gun facing ahead.
  The code for WoL's DLL is in the porting pages (turretArc).

2. GatlingCamelDeployed no longer dismounts
- The camel lies down with the gunner in the saddle, and he fires from there, the gun turning a full 90 degrees
  each way. Deploying now takes 3.33 s and undeploying 2.17 s (the new animation lengths, set in
  GatlingCamelDeployed.tactics).

3. The new reload (both units)
- He lifts the empty magazine off, throws it aside (it falls and sinks out of sight), pulls a full one from a
  pocket at his left hip and seats it. The reload is still 3 s, so the rate of fire does not change.

Everything is in wol-fixes/gatling-camel (apply_fix.py with --check and --restore; README updated).

Tested (single player): targets on both sides and behind, a series of targets one after another, deploying and
undeploying while aiming, a game saved and loaded while aiming, deaths while aiming (the corpse shows the gun at
rest), 30 camels firing at once (no performance cost measured), and a skirmish against an AI with the pack (the
AI plays normally).

Videos: gatlingCamel-wol (the gun turning, from the saddle), gatlingCamelDeployed-wol (deployed, gunner in the
saddle), gatlingCamel-move-wol (walking), turretArc-wol-control (the same scene with the key off: the whole camel
turns).

One more thing about the art already in the pack (rounds 2 and 3): it has two faults that the next
version fixes in every animation. The gunner's forearm can turn over in a single frame (idle, walk, jog,
run, fire, deploy, reload), and at the jog and the run the gun slides 13 to 24 cm off its post. The next
version replaces every art file of the pack, so please don't ship the current art on its own.
