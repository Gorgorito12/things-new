Reply to JTMaston and Punchline, round of 2026-11-23
=====================================================

Everything below was tested in single player in a copy of WoL, and filmed or pictured with a control where it
shows on screen. The guide sections and docs/wol-requests.md have the details, the videos and the pictures.


#78 "NEW" Levant crash: found and fixed
---------------------------------------
The crash is not in the map script. It is the game's 3D model cache: the exe has a fixed 30 MB budget for loaded
models, and every frame it unloads the least recently used model while it is over that, even if units on the map
still use it. WoL's art keeps the cache over 30 MB from the first minutes (584 to 1,751 unloads in one game), so
sooner or later a unit draws a model that was freed under it and the game crashes inside Granny (Windows reports
age3y.exe offset 0000c07c / 0000cc7b, or granny2.dll offset 0003a02a / 0002c8cd / 0002c9a4). Big games make it
more likely. It also crashed with only your uhc.dll and none of ours, so it is the game, not a DLL.

A second, smaller source is in the data: a damage template that names a "part" bone the anim file does not
define, or that the model does not have. The first time the game meets such a bone in a game, it reloads that
model while units still draw it. One game crashed this way (a cypress using the oak's damage template).

The fix (we recommend all three):
1. modelCacheLimit 0 (new key in our DLL): no model is ever unloaded for the budget. Without our DLL, patch
   age3y.exe yourselves: file offset 0x6C043 and file offset 0x2E017A, 80 C3 C9 01 -> FF FF FF FF (check both
   hold 80 C3 C9 01 first). Cost: about 100-150 MB more memory in a 13-minute game; your exe is
   large-address-aware, so that is fine.
2. dmgPartBoneFix (new key): when a damage part names a bone the model cannot use, the game no longer reloads
   the model; that debris piece is just not drawn on that model. This covers every such case, yours, the
   original game's and future art. Without our DLL it is one byte: file offset 0x258C1A, 74 -> EB (check that
   0x258C18 holds 84 DB 74 1E first).
3. The data pack wol-fixes/art-dmg-bones-20261123 (WoL-dmg-bones-fix.zip, laid out like the game folder, or
   apply_fix.py with backup and restore): it adds 1533 missing <definebone> lines in 131 anim files, and fixes
   the cypress (the oak's template removed from its death component) and the markets. Nothing else changes.
   After it our checker finds 0 undefined part bones. 179 other model/template pairs use a template whose bones
   the model does not have at all; a definebone line cannot fix those. They are listed by template in
   missing-bones-by-template.txt (most common: house_generic_a.dmg and nat_corral_age2_damaged.dmg on 11
   models each, tree_madrone_a.dmg on 9). With dmgPartBoneFix they cannot crash; fix them when you touch those
   files.

Tested: 6-player and 8-player games on "NEW" Levant, free for all and 4v4, Hard and Expert, European AIs, one
player clicking every unit on the revealed map, 13.5-14 minutes each: 0 crashes with the fix and the cache
never unloaded a model during play. Before the fix the same test crashed in about half the games. Full report:
wol-fixes/maps/levant-fix-20261123.txt.


#84 Dismount: no melee on foot
------------------------------
The dismounted Chasseur no longer fights in melee. On foot its stance has only two attacks: the same shot it
has mounted (same damage, range, rate of fire, bonuses and tech upgrades) and the torch against buildings. The
shot's minimum range is now 0, so it keeps firing at point blank when an enemy reaches it; before, inside 2 m
it could not shoot and switched to a bayonet attack. Tested: five dismounted chasseurs against three charging
Scythemen kept shooting in contact and won, with no melee animation; mounted, the same fight is sabres. Also
against Hussars, the torch on a House, save/load on foot, both horse modes, and the AI compiles.
Question: the torch stays because it only targets buildings; should we remove it too? If another unit should
fight hand to hand on foot, the tool can still make that animation (recipe option "melee").


#92 displayedRange: no white border, more contrast, and readable rings
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The white border is gone. Each range ring is one thin line (0.5 m) in the owner's full colour, flat like the
game's own selection circles. The pale tint came from the ring's bump map, which asked for shine; it is flat
now, so blue reads as a real blue.
We also looked at how the Definitive Edition shows ranges (one faint ring per unit) and kept one ring per attack,
because a single ring would hide, for example, the Outpost's 24 m attack behind its 40 m anti-ship one. How to
read them now:
- Colour = owner: yours, an ally's or an enemy's.
- Fill = where that attack hits you: a soft fill in the owner's colour, clear near the unit and stronger toward
  the ring. Where several attacks reach, the fills add up, so the zone covered by more attacks looks stronger.
- Dashed ring with no fill = ships only (read from the attack's target types, not its name): for example the
  Outpost's and the Fort's 40 m anti-ship attack.
A building's placement ghost shows the lines only. Cost: about 0.5 ms a frame with 15 units' rings on screen
(the cap), and no lag spikes. To update, copy the new tools/rings folder over art/effects/uhc_rings.
Known limit: with the team-colour option the fill keeps each player's own colour.


#97 Resendable cards (new key resendableCards)
----------------------------------------------
Put <resendeveryage>1</resendeveryage> in the <card> of homecity<Civ>.xml. Each time the player reaches a new
age, the card gets its sends back. The count is reset, never added, so a card sent in Age II and not sent in
Age III has one send in Age IV, not two.
A note on infiniteinlastage: in the exe it does not make a card unlimited. It refills the card's sends once
when the player reaches the Imperial Age. With both tags, the Imperial Age is just one more age-up.
Tested with Dutch: cards sent in Ages I to IV came back at each age-up; a skipped age did not stack; untagged
cards stayed used; saved games keep the state.
Questions:
1. We give back the card's full <maxcount> at each age-up (a card that can be sent twice gets both sends back).
   Do you want that, or only one send per age?
2. Should a tagged card's rollover say so, e.g. "This Card can be sent again in each new Age"? If yes, give us
   the string ID.
3. We could not see the AI resend a tagged card in our short test games. It reads the same data, but tell us if
   your AI scripts need anything.


#98 Retargeting inside an attack (new key retargetInAction)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Tag: <RetargetInAction>1</RetargetInAction> on an attack action. The Maxim's "bullets" are the six Attack tags
of its Bombard_ranged_attack animation, each a full shot. When the target died, the game put the attack into
its reload wait, so the remaining tags of that animation fired nothing until the 4 s ROF was over. With the
tag, a unit whose target dies while it is still in its attack animation immediately takes the nearest enemy in
range that it could attack on its own, and the remaining shots of the same animation hit it. Nothing restarts:
same cycle, same ROF. If no enemy is in range, nothing changes. It also works with numberOfShots bursts.
Lag: the search only runs when a tagged unit loses its target; with 30 Maxims and 10 burst riflemen against
100 infantry the simulation time per frame was the same (0.36 against 0.35 ms). Put the tag on CannonAttack in
organGun.tactics (it covers the Maxim and the 6 other guns that share it), and on gwMaxim if you want it there.
Questions:
1. If the player ordered the attack on a specific target and it dies, the gun also switches to the nearest
   enemy. OK, or should only automatic targets switch?
2. Balance: all six shots now land, so against groups the Maxim deals much more (2 Maxims beat 16 Regulars that
   win without the tag). Do you want to lower its damage?
3. Target choice is the nearest enemy in range. Would you prefer the one nearest the dead target or the weakest?
4. With numberOfShots, a target that dies during the reload wait between bursts still makes the next burst
   start on the unit's own next target. Is that fine?


#99 Quick arrival card (new key quickArrivalCards)
--------------------------------------------------
<Flag>QuickArrival</Flag> on the card's tech. The card's rollover gets the bullet line "This is a quick-arrival
card.", in the same style as the other lines: right after "This Card may be sent INFINITE times" for infinite
cards, and after the "ONE time / N times" line for the others, which places it between the rollover text and
the advanced info. It works in the shipment panel and in Build a Deck. quickArrivalCards stringid N takes the
text from your string table so you can translate it. Nothing else about the card changes.
Question: is it fine that the line comes after the "may be sent ONE time" line on non-infinite cards?

#100 Slinger animations on other models (for Punchline)
-------------------------------------------------------
We found why the Macehualtin's animations only play on that model, and fixed it in the art; no DLL change is
needed. The game only starts an animation on a model if the animation file contains a track group with that
model's exact name. The Macehualtin's model and all 35 of its animations are named "bone_master", while every
standard infantry model is named "Bip01". So on any other body the animations do nothing and the unit stays
frozen in its bind pose; that is also why both of WoL's current slingers reuse the Macehualtin model. The
skeletons are otherwise the same biped, and the sling is a separate attachment that works on any body.
We retargeted all 35 animations onto the standard Bip01 skeleton, each with its tag file, so the stone leaves
from the same point. The pack wol-fixes/slinger-anims adds two sets (one made on WoL's Skirmisher body, one on
the native Rifleman body; either works on any standard-proportion Bip01 body) and the ready-to-paste anim file
blocks; it changes none of your files. For a model with other proportions or bone names, our tool can make a
set for that model too. Tested in game with test slingers on both bodies: idle, walk, sling attack in step with
your Polynesian slinger, melee, deaths, save/load; the AI still compiles.
Questions:
1. Which slinger units are you planning, and which models will they use?
2. Should the new slingers keep the Macehualtin's shield? The blocks leave it out by default.
3. Which projectile will they use? The Macehualtin and POLYMarauder use "Arrow"; a dedicated sling-stone
   projectile is possible.
