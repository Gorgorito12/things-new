About the gatling camel (#102): the new model is done and tested in game. It replaces the current one.

What it is
- The zamburak's camel with its own swivel gun removed, carrying the American Gatling's barrel cluster, its casing
  with the trunnion, the crank and the magazine on its back (at 0.55 of the Gatling's size). The rider turns the
  crank: the barrels spin and the crank turns while it fires, the shots and the muzzle flashes leave from the
  muzzle, and on reload he swaps the magazine. The camel lowers its head out of the line of fire.
- Two units, as you'll see in the videos:
  1. GatlingCamel (your existing unit, same stats, cost, cards and strings): now fires from the saddle and moves
     while it fights, like the zamburak (no deploying). Only its anim file and tactics change.
  2. GatlingCamelDeployed (new, a copy of GatlingCamel): the camel lies down, the gunner gets off step by step and
     fires on foot, then gets back on before the camel stands up. It keeps the deploy tactics; deploying takes
     5.33 s and undeploying 4.17 s (the length of the animations).
- Everything is in wol-fixes/gatling-camel (apply_fix.py, with --check and --restore; README inside). Your current
  KingsReturn gatling camel files are not touched.

Tested (single player): both units idle, walking, firing, reloading, deploying, dying, save/load, and a skirmish
against a German AI with the pack applied (no XS errors).

Questions
1. How should players get the deployed unit (GatlingCamelDeployed)? Same as the current one (the Vickers Purchase
   card, HCGatlingCamels1, the Hookah Lounge), a separate card, or an upgrade/tech that switches between the two?
   Right now nothing trains it.
2. Stats: the deployed unit is an exact copy of GatlingCamel (340 HP, 500 gold, range 22, ROF 4, 4 population).
   Do you want it different, e.g. longer range or more damage while deployed, and is the saddle version fine with
   the same numbers now that it fires on the move?
3. Should the old model stay available as an option (for example for a later age or a civ), or can it go?
4. Gun size: we used 0.55 of the Gatling; 0.70 is also ready if you prefer a bigger gun.

Videos: gatlingCamel-wol (from the saddle), gatlingCamelDeployed-wol (deployed), gatlingCamel-move-wol (both
units walking), gatlingCamel-wol-control (the current unit).

A note on movement: the anim file has its own walk, jog and run (both hands on the reins at the run), all checked
in game. In our tests both units used the walk at every speed we gave them, so the jog and run only
show where the game picks them.
