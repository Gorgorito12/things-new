Hi JTMaston,

GarrisonConvertTo, version 2, built from your answers of 2026-11-26. The key is the same, one line in
Startup\wol.cfg:

    garrisonConvert

(add "log" to see each conversion in UHC.log). The old tags keep working as they are.

1. What changed
- eject: <GarrisonConvertTo eject="true">Musketeer</GarrisonConvertTo> puts each unit out of the building as
  soon as it is converted, the way the building's own eject command does it. eject="1" and eject="" work too;
  eject="false", eject="0" or no attribute keep it inside. (A bare "eject" with no value is not valid XML, so
  please write eject="true".)
- Only hit points are kept: the unit keeps its share of HP, as a TransformUnit effect does.
- Population: a conversion that would go over the population cap waits. The progress pie stays full, and the
  player sees the game's own message, the one a Town Center shows when it cannot train:
  "No population room for Hussar; training will be suspended." with its sound, once per pause. When there is
  room again, the conversion goes on by itself. The check is the target's population minus the unit's, with
  the usual AllowOverPopCap and AlwaysAllowOverPopCap flags.
- Building destroyed: the units come out as they are (the one in progress unconverted).
- Only the owner's units are converted (allies' units are left alone).
- Several tags take turns (round robin). The rule: each building keeps a cursor, at the first tag at the start.
  For each conversion it takes the first tag, from the cursor on (wrapping around), whose "from" matches the
  unit; the unit converts with that tag's target, delay and eject, and the cursor moves to the next tag.
  Example, with
      <GarrisonConvertTo from="Settler" delay="3">Pikeman</GarrisonConvertTo>
      <GarrisonConvertTo delay="3" eject="true">Musketeer</GarrisonConvertTo>
  Settler, Settler, Missionary, Settler give Pikeman (stays inside), Musketeer (walks out), Musketeer (walks
  out: the Missionary is not a Settler, so it skips the first tag), Pikeman (stays inside). The cursor is kept
  in saved games.
- from: from="Settler" (a unit) or from="AbstractVillager" (a unit type) converts only those units. Without it,
  every unit of the owner converts.
- A unit that already is the target of one of the building's tags is never converted again (so the tags above
  do not turn the Pikemen inside into Musketeers).
- Delay: one at a time, in the order they entered, as before. The pie as before.

2. Techs (Data effects on a ProtoUnit target, per player, like the stock Data subtypes)
The optional attribute tag="N" picks the N-th GarrisonConvertTo of the building (in the order of the data, then
the ones techs added); without it the effect changes every tag.

    <!-- every tag of the Town Center: delay 2 s (Assign, Absolute, BasePercent and Percent all work) -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="2" subtype="GarrisonConvertDelay" relativity="Assign">
        <Target type="ProtoUnit">TownCenter</Target></Effect>
    <!-- only the 2nd tag; or only the tags that convert to Pikeman: unittype="Pikeman" -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="2" subtype="GarrisonConvertDelay" relativity="Assign" tag="2">...</Effect>
    <!-- eject on (1) or off (0) -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="1" subtype="GarrisonConvertEject" relativity="Assign">...</Effect>
    <!-- the target of the 1st tag becomes Halberdier -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="0" subtype="GarrisonConvertTarget" relativity="Assign" unittype="Halberdier" tag="1">...</Effect>
    <!-- from: a unit or a unit type; without unittype the tags take every unit again -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="0" subtype="GarrisonConvertFrom" relativity="Assign" unittype="AbstractVillager">...</Effect>
    <!-- a new tag at the end: converts to Grenadier; amount = its delay in seconds -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="0" subtype="GarrisonConvertAdd" relativity="Assign" unittype="Grenadier">...</Effect>
    <!-- removes the tags that convert to Hussar (or, with tag="N", that tag) -->
    <Effect type="Data" amount="0" subtype="GarrisonConvertRemove" relativity="Assign" unittype="Hussar">...</Effect>

A tech can give a building tags it did not have in protoy.xml (GarrisonConvertAdd).

3. Limits
- When a tech is taken away (a deactivated tech), Delay and Eject go back and Add removes its tag, but Target,
  From and Remove stay as the tech left them.
- A tag is picked by its number or by its target only.
- A saved game applies the techs again when it loads, in the order of the techtree, not the order they were
  researched. If one tech adds a tag and another tech then changes it with tag="N", put both effects in one
  tech, or don't count on the numbers.
- Up to 32 tags per building.

4. Tested in game (WoL, Dutch against a British AI): the round robin with from and eject, from with a unit type,
the building killed mid-way, save and load in the middle of a conversion and with the cursor on the 2nd tag, the
population pause with the message and the resume, a tech changing delay, target, eject and from and adding and
removing tags (and a save/load after it), bad values; the AI plays normally and the performance is the same with
the key on and off. The videos and the full rules are in the guide, section garrisonConvert.
