Reply to JTMaston, round of 2026-12-02: the guerrilla cooldown as an attribute, and a tech for it (request #114)
===============================================================================================================

Thanks for the message of 2026-11-28. Two changes:

1. The cooldown is an attribute of <PartisanNearby>, not a new tag.

       <PartisanNearby cooldown="60">30</PartisanNearby>

   30 is the radius in metres, as before. cooldown is seconds of game time, 0 to 3600; left out, or 0: no limit.
   It works as the tag of the last version did: when a building gives its guerrilla, it gives nothing again until
   that many seconds have passed; each building has its own wait; a building that is still waiting is not restarted
   by the next destruction; the wait is kept in saved games.
   A wrong value ("abc", "-5", "99999", empty) is written to the log and ignored, and the radius still works.
   The <PartisanCooldown> tag of the last version is no longer read (it was never in a release). If your data still
   has it, the log says once that it is now the cooldown attribute.

2. A tech can change the cooldown (your teammate Punchline's suggestion).
   A new Data subtype, PartisanCooldown, on a building, the same way as PartisanCount:

       <Effect type ='Data' amount ='0.50' subtype ='PartisanCooldown' relativity ='Percent'>
           <Target type ='ProtoUnit'>Barracks</Target></Effect>

   - Assign sets the seconds, Absolute adds seconds (a negative amount takes them away), Percent multiplies.
   - It starts from the civ's cooldown attribute, and the result stays between 0 and 3600 seconds.
   - It changes only the player who gets the tech, and only that building. An abstract type such as Building as the
     target changes all of that player's buildings.
   - A saved game applies the tech again when it loads.
   - A building remembers WHEN it last gave its guerrilla, not how long it has left. So a tech that shortens the wait
     also frees the buildings that are already waiting.
     Example: a Barracks gives at 1:50 with a 60 s cooldown. The tech above (x 0.5, so 30 s) comes at 2:00. At 2:22,
     32 s after it gave, the Barracks gives again.

Example, for the Spanish:

    civs.xml, in the Spanish <civ>:
        <PartisanNearby cooldown="60">30</PartisanNearby>
    techtreey.xml, a card or tech that halves the wait of the Barracks:
        <Effect type ='Data' amount ='0.50' subtype ='PartisanCooldown' relativity ='Percent'>
            <Target type ='ProtoUnit'>Barracks</Target></Effect>

The pack wol-fixes/guerrilla/:
    python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>" --nearby 30 --cooldown 60
                                                       writes <PartisanNearby cooldown="60">30</PartisanNearby> for
                                                       the Spanish (and takes out an old <PartisanCooldown> line)
    python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>" --check         what is applied, the cooldown too
    python apply_fix.py "<WoL folder>" --restore       everything back as it was (protoy.xml, civs.xml, the cfg)

Tested in game (Spanish against the Serbian AI, units counted after each event):
- The attribute gives the same times as the tag of the last version: a House next to a Barracks and a Stable, 2 + 2
  Rodeleros; 20 s later another House, nothing ("on cooldown, 40.0 s left" for each); after a save and a load,
  "10.0 s left"; 70 s after they gave, 2 + 2 again. A Market gave its 3 while the Barracks beside it was waiting.
- The tech (x 0.5 on Barracks) while a Barracks and a Stable were both waiting (they gave at 1:50, the tech came at
  2:00): at 2:22 the Barracks gave its 2, the Stable gave nothing ("28.0 s left").
- The same game saved and loaded after the tech: the tech still counted ("7.0 s left" for the Barracks, 30 s since it
  gave; "5.0 s left" for the Stable, 60 s), and 10 s later both gave.
- The same with our DLL next to WoL's own uhc.dll.
- Wrong values and the old tag: one line each in the log. Key off: nothing. The Serbian AI kept training units.

The GIFs of the last version (partisans-cooldown-wol and its control) still show what happens: only where the number
is written has changed.

A reminder: the Serbians still have SerbianInferno (<partisans>1</partisans> and the last PartisanType of those
buildings). The pack does not touch them, and they are not in the nearby mode, so their buildings still give their
own guerrilla when they fall.
